# How to use the lab

Two voices. About ninety seconds when read aloud. Drop an mp3 at `audio/how-to-use.mp3` and the home player will pick it up. espeak-ng and piper were not available in this build, so the clip was not generated.

**A:** You are on the Webex Lab Free University. The first screen is the orbit. The buttons under the title are the doors.

**B:** Diagrams opens the atlas. Flows opens the animated call paths. Click a drawing and it fills the page. Pause, play, or step the packet. Charts are counts from this repository, with the source in the caption.

**A:** Cheat Codes is the one-page sheets, the card deck, and the offline zip. Play is the ports game and the daily challenge. Ecosystem is how Calling, Contact Center, Meetings, devices, and Control Hub sit together. Downloads is the same bundle. Careers is the cert map, and it does not invent salaries.

**B:** Themes recolor the orbit. The language menu changes the labels on this desk, not the seventy-eight rooms. Arabic flips the reading direction.

**A:** Read a room, then come back. Product claims stay in the room that cites them.
