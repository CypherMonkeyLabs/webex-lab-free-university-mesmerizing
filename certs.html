Cert tracker

CCNP Collaboration is the core exam plus one concentration. The topic phrases below are the official blurbs retrieved 2026-10-14. This page does not invent domain weights or percentages. Links go to Canon rooms that practice the phrase, not to a promise that the room covers the blueprint.

How to use this page

Read the room the topic points at. If the room still has a TODO-verify, you do not recite that cell as a fact. The 30/60/90 plan on Start here is a Canon study order, not Cisco’s training path.

350-801 CLCOR

Implementing and Operating Cisco Collaboration Core Technologies. 120 minutes, $US400, English. Passing it earns Cisco Certified Specialist - Collaboration Core and can meet the core requirement for CCNP Collaboration and CCIE Collaboration. Topics on the exam overview: infrastructure and design, protocols, endpoints, Cisco IOS XE gateway and media resources, call control, QoS, and collaboration applications.

300-815 CLACC

Implementing Cisco Advanced Call Control On-Premises. 90 minutes, $US300. Specialist: Collaboration Call Control & Mobility Implementation. CLACCM retired February 2, 2026. Topics on the exam overview: signaling and media protocols, CME/SRST, Cisco Unified Border Element, call control and dial planning, UCM call control, and mobility.

300-820 CLHCT

90 minutes, $US300. Specialist: Collaboration Cloud & Edge Implementation. CLCEI retired February 2, 2026. Retrieved 2026-10-14, the exam page’s overview sentence is “Implementing Cisco Collaboration Cloud and Edge Solutions (300-820 CLHCT).” The same page’s title line is “Implementing Cisco Collaboration Hybrid and Cloud Technologies.” Quote the sentence you are looking at. Topics in the overview: collaboration cloud technologies, suite and device configuration, cloud management, suite and devices management and administration, security, and APIs and programmability.
