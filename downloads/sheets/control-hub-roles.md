[cite:1] T1-R01-control-hub-foundations.html Why does it exist — what breaks without a single org console? Services, identities, and devices would be administered as separate products. The overview exists so an admin sees which services are actually provisioned before configuring them. [cite:1] [cite:5] T1-R01-control-hub-foundations.html What breaks if only one person holds full administrator? Cisco recommends more than one full administrator so the org stays operable. The partner’s administrator is the first full administrator and is expected to create more. [cite:2] T1-R01-control-hub-foundations.html How does an admin action work end to end? Sign in, change a user or location or device, and the org record is what later services read. Role grants can take up to 10 minutes. A device is not in the org until its activation code is used. [cite:2] [cite:1] T1-R01-control-hub-foundations.html Where do licenses sit relative to users? The Get Started article says the overview is where you view licenses across services and administer licenses to users. Organization-based and group-based license assignment are full-admin privileges in the role matrix. The SKU catalog is T1 T1-R01-control-hub-foundations.html Are locations only a calling idea? No. The locations article says you can use one location for both calling and workspaces, and that settings templates can apply to groups and locations.
