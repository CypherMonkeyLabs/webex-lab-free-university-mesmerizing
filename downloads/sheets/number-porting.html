Number porting and cutover

One page pulled from the Canon rooms named in the source column. Open the room before you quote a number in a design. This is not a copy of a Cisco document.

CheckWhat the room saysSource
What is a port?A transfer from the losing carrier to the winning carrier. [cite:1]T6-R06-number-porting-cutover.html
What two documents prove you may port?A bill from the last 30 days and an LOA or eLOA. [cite:1]T6-R06-number-porting-cutover.html
How many numbers does one license allow?One. The port count cannot exceed purchased licenses. [cite:1]T6-R06-number-porting-cutover.html
When is the requested date real?When the losing carrier returns an FOC. Until then it is a request. [cite:1]T6-R06-number-porting-cutover.html
What happens to numbers on the same account?They port together, even if they were typed on different orders. [cite:1]T6-R06-number-porting-cutover.html
How do you prove you can still edit the order?FOC has not arrived. After FOC, Control Hub edits stop and PTS is the path. [cite:1]T6-R06-number-porting-cutover.html
What evidence shows a port-out was ignored?One business day passed on PSTN & Routing → PSTN Orders. The article says the request is then accepted automatically. [cite:2]T6-R06-number-porting-cutover.html
