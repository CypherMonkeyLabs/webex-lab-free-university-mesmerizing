Ecosystem atlas

Calling, Contact Center, Meetings, Devices, Control Hub, workforce management, recording, emergency calling, analytics, and AI agents. Diagrams are study drawings. They are not Cisco architecture posters.

Control Hub

The organization is the boundary for users, roles, locations, and services. A person you add starts without administrative privileges. T1-R01. Control Hub article.

Webex Calling

Each location picks Cisco Calling Plan, Cloud Connected PSTN, or premises PSTN on a Local Gateway. Phones do not register through that gateway. T2-R01 T2-R02.

Devices

Claim by MAC or activation code. Firmware and activation use the device-configuration ports in the Calling port reference, including TCP 443 and 6970. T2-R04.

Meetings

The Calling port reference tells suite customers to also allow the separate network-requirements article for Meetings and messaging. This atlas does not copy that matrix. Meeting UDP ranges are not restated here. b2exve.

Webex Contact Center

Multi-tenant: entry point, flow, queue, team. Enterprise is a different product with Router, Logger, PG, and CUIC. T3-R01 T4-R01.

Workforce management

Forecast, schedule, and adherence live with WFO, not with Calling CDR. Article py6u9h scopes the listed WFO SKUs to Enterprise, CCE, and CCX. Multi-tenant applicability is unverified. T3-R09.

Recording and quality

Select the Calling provider before you enable users. Webex recordings: one year, 100 GB. Dubber Go: 30 days. Recording disables Calling end-to-end encryption. Quality-management SKUs are a contact-center add-on, not that toggle. T2-R06.

E911

Port 443 to api.wxc.e911cloud.com and wxc.e911cloud.com. Test with 933. Do not prove the design by calling emergency services. Ports page.

Analytics

Control Hub analytics retention and the Calling troubleshooting view are different clocks. The Canon records a 21-day troubleshooting window and a longer analytics window for Pro Pack. Re-check both live articles before you quote a retention number in a design. T1-R06 T2-R08 T3-R07.

AI agents

Virtual Agent–Voice, AI Assistant, and AI Agent Studio are different surfaces. A bring-your-own virtual agent escalates with AGENT_TRANSFER. T8-R05.

Integrations and future hooks

Published connectors: Salesforce Lightning, Dynamics CIF, ServiceNow. No customer logo wall is drawn here, because this lab does not invent deployments. A future hook is a webhook or connector you have not turned on: recording events, Connect flows, and CRM screen pop. Build from the article, not from a peer-product column. T8-R03 T8-R04.
