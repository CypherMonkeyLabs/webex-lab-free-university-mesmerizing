Career path in this lab

A calling administrator who can defend a trunk, a dial plan, and 933 is useful on a Calling project. A contact-center engineer adds flows, queues, and Analyzer. Enterprise adds ICM script sense and CUIC. Dedicated Instance adds peering. Certifications are optional evidence. The Canon cert page records CLCOR plus one of CLACC, CLHCT, or CLCCE. Domain weights are not printed, because memorizing a percent is not the skill.
