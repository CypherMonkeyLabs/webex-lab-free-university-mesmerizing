00 · Header

T1 · Control Hub & Platform Foundations

Admin Roles & Partner Admin

People you add start with no administrative privileges. A full administrator can assign roles, and it may take up to 10 minutes before the new role is usable. Location administrator is exclusive. User and device administrator, and device administrator, cannot administer Webex Calling. A Webex Calling administrator can be assigned up to 250 locations.

Room T1-R05Version v0.1last-verified: 2026-10-14Difficulty L2Est. time 80–100 minPrereq T1-R01Reading 22 min

Key takeaways

  • On first setup, the administrator from the partner organization becomes the full administrator and can create more administrators. [cite:1]
  • Full administrators cannot simultaneously hold location administrator. The article prints that as “simultenously.” Read-only cannot be combined with a custom role or location administrator. [cite:1]
  • Organizations can have up to 100 groups with assigned administrator roles. One group can hold up to 20 different roles. You cannot manage administrator roles through the administrator CSV. [cite:1]
  • Full administrators cannot assign Advanced Troubleshooting Access to themselves. Another full administrator assigns it. The role can go to a full, read-only, or support administrator. [cite:1]
  • Location administrators cannot add or delete locations, cannot modify the PSTN connection, and cannot use bulk operations. Adding administrators to one location allows up to 5 users at a time. The delegation drop-down selects up to five locations. [cite:2]

01 · Executive summary

Five lines, then why it matters

  • Assign a person’s roles at Management > Users > the user > Summary > Administrator roles. Assign a group’s roles at Groups > Webex groups > the group > Assignments > Assign roles. [cite:1]
  • User and device administrators can assign the location administrator role and cannot assign other administrator roles. Device administrators register devices, generate activation codes, and manage workspaces. Neither can administer Webex Calling. [cite:1]
  • Compliance officers are the role with access to user-generated content and legal hold. Security activity administrators are the other role, with full administrators, marked for the admin actions audit log. [cite:1]
  • The earlier Webex Calling Detailed Call History API access role is marked Obsolete and remains operational until December 31, 2026. New assignments use a custom role. [cite:1]
  • A location administrator can delete hunt-group agents from other locations and can add new hunt-group users only from assigned locations. For a Webex Calling call queue they can add users from other locations, and they can allow join or unjoin only for agents in assigned locations. [cite:2]
Why it matters. Interview answers that say “admin” fail here. Partner launch, location scope, and the Calling restriction on device roles are three different keys.

Tip

Changing the organizational role of a user who already has a services role can affect that services role. Review services roles first. [cite:1]

Gotcha

A user with a directly assigned read-only or location administrator role will not inherit an incompatible role from a group. Remove the direct role, remove the user from the group, and add them to the group again. [cite:1]

Glossary

Full administrator
Access to every aspect of how the organization is run. Exclusive of location administrator. [cite:1]
Location administrator
Delegated administration of certain services in assigned locations. Exclusive of other administrator roles. [cite:1] [cite:2]

Live Meeting access. A full administrator cannot assign it to themselves. [cite:1]

The organization role marked for user-generated content and legal hold. [cite:1]
Services role
A role such as a Webex site role that can be affected when the organizational role changes. [cite:1]
Obsolete CDR role
Webex Calling Detailed Call History API access, marked Obsolete, operational until December 31, 2026. [cite:1]
Partner Hub
The console the role article names for administrators who manage multiple customer organizations. [cite:1]
Provisioning administrator
In the contact-center table, a partner-organization role that can perform contact-center administrative activities a partner full administrator can perform, and cannot perform user management. [cite:1]

06 · Config

Click paths

  1. Sign in to Control Hub. Management > Users. Open the user. Summary tab, Administrator roles, select roles, Save. [cite:1]
  2. If you are a partner, open the customer organization first, then that organization’s Users page. [cite:1]
  3. Review services roles before you change the organizational role. [cite:1]
  4. For a group: Groups > Webex groups > the group > Assignments > Assign roles > Save. [cite:1]
  5. Find administrators: Management > Users > Filter, check the roles. Export from the top right. Do not change the filter until the CSV finishes. [cite:1]
  6. Location admin from the user: Summary > Administrator roles > Delegation > Location admin > up to five locations > Done. The delegation article also shows the Profile tab path. [cite:2]
  7. Location admin from the location: Management > Locations > the location > Admins > Add. Up to 5 users at a time. [cite:2]
  8. Remove a location from an administrator by leaving Location admin checked and removing that location, or uncheck Location admin to remove the role. [cite:2]
  9. Advanced troubleshooting access: another full administrator assigns it to a full, read-only, or support administrator. [cite:1]
  10. For new Calling detailed-call-history API access, create the custom role and assign it from Users. The functional role stays operational until December 31, 2026. [cite:1]

09 · Self-question ladder

Twelve rungs

L0What privileges does a new user have?

None. [cite:1]

L0Who becomes full administrator on first setup?

The administrator from the partner organization. [cite:1]

L1Why is location administrator exclusive?

The role article says you can’t assign any other administrator role to a location administrator, and a full administrator can’t hold it at the same time. [cite:1]

L1How long can a new role take?

Up to 10 minutes. [cite:1]

L2Who can assign roles to users?

The matrix marks full administrator as yes, and read-only as read-only. User and device administrators can assign the location administrator role and cannot assign other administrator roles. [cite:1]

L2Who sees the admin actions audit log?

Full administrator and security activity administrator. [cite:1]

L3How many groups can carry admin roles?

Up to 100. One group can have up to 20 roles. [cite:1]

L3What is the location scale for a Calling administrator?

Up to 250 assigned locations. The drop-down in one save is up to five. One location can receive up to 5 administrators at a time. [cite:2]

L4Can a location administrator add a hunt-group agent from another site?

They can delete any assigned agent, including agents from other locations, and they can add new users only from their assigned locations. [cite:2]

L4Can a customer read-only administrator open Contact Center admin?

No. The table says they cannot access the Webex Contact Center administrative interfaces. [cite:1]

L5What replaces the obsolete Calling CDR role?

A custom role for the Detailed Call History CDR APIs, including stream and feed. The old role remains operational until December 31, 2026. [cite:1]

L5Can the administrator CSV change roles?

No. You can’t manage administrator roles through the CSV file. [cite:1]

13 · Interview

Oral answers

30 seconds. New users have no admin rights. Full administrator runs the organization and cannot also be a location administrator. Device administrator and user-and-device administrator cannot administer Webex Calling. A new role can take up to 10 minutes.

2 minutes. I assign roles on the user summary or on a Webex group. One organization can have 100 groups with roles, and one group can hold 20 roles. Read-only does not combine with a custom role. A direct read-only or location role blocks an incompatible group role until I remove it and re-add the user to the group. Compliance officers get user content and legal hold. Security activity administrators share the audit log with full administrators. I cannot grant myself Advanced Troubleshooting Access.

30 seconds, locations. Location administrator is exclusive. I can pick up to five locations in the drop-down, add up to five people to one location at a time, and a Webex Calling administrator can hold up to 250 assigned locations. That 250 is not an organization-wide cap.

2 minutes, the fence. A location administrator cannot add or delete locations, cannot modify PSTN, cannot run bulk operations, and cannot add a device from the devices screen. They can add devices on a user or a workspace. On a hunt group they can delete any agent and add users only from their own locations. On a Calling call queue they can add users from other locations and can allow join or unjoin only inside their locations.

What you will say about the old CDR role. It is marked Obsolete and stays operational until December 31, 2026. New access is a custom role for the detailed call history APIs. Partner Hub role names stay in article 16gmxfb, which this pass did not re-read line by line.
