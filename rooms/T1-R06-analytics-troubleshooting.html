T1 · Control Hub and platform

Analytics & Troubleshooting Views

Three different clocks live in Control Hub: 21 days of call troubleshooting, 3 or 13 months of analytics, and 30 days of uploaded client logs. Mixing them up is how a ticket goes looking in the wrong room.

Room T1-R06 Version v0.1 last-verified: TODO Difficulty L2 Est. time 70–90 min Prereq T1-R01 Reading 15 min

Key takeaways

  • Webex Calling troubleshooting is Monitoring → Troubleshooting. It keeps 21 days and shows segments, hops, and whether media used the cloud or ICE. [cite:1]
  • Path optimization is ICE, PNC, or No optimization. A CSV holds 100 records from the tab you are on. [cite:1]
  • Analytics keeps 3 months, or 13 with Pro Pack. From January 27, 2026 you select 3 months at a time even when the longer history is still there. [cite:2]
  • The worst-experience table cross-launches into Troubleshooting, still capped at 21 days. [cite:2]

01 · Executive summary

Five lines, then why it matters

  • Webex Calling troubleshooting is Monitoring → Troubleshooting. It keeps 21 days and shows segments, hops, and whether media used the cloud or ICE. [cite:1]
  • Path optimization is ICE, PNC, or No optimization. A CSV holds 100 records from the tab you are on. [cite:1]
  • Analytics keeps 3 months, or 13 with Pro Pack. From January 27, 2026 you select 3 months at a time even when the longer history is still there. [cite:2]
  • The worst-experience table cross-launches into Troubleshooting, still capped at 21 days. [cite:2]
  • Client logs are Troubleshooting → Logs, kept 30 days, and only if the user is online. [cite:3]
Why it matters. “Pull the logs” and “pull the CDR” are different pages with different retention. Saying 13 months when you mean the 21-day view is a wrong answer.

03 · Core concepts

Search behavior that surprises people

A segment is not the whole story

The article says each segment can succeed, fail, or be refused. You read the segment that matches the complaint, then the hop. [cite:1]

Email search splits the world

Searching a user’s email puts meetings and calls on separate tabs. A correlation ID or a phone number shows only the relevant tab. [cite:1]

History is not the picker

Pro Pack storing 13 months does not mean the picker spans 13 months. The article’s January 27, 2026 change limits a selection to 3 months. Today is after that date. re-check that the control matches the sentence. [cite:2]

Not a bill

Analytics is explicitly not for billing, and it is not there for Webex Online sites on *.my.webex.com. [cite:2]

04 · Ecosystem & sister technologies

Glossary, map, sisters, edges

Glossary

Troubleshooting view
The Control Hub place where you search one Webex Calling call, see whether it succeeded, refused, or failed, and open media statistics. [cite:1]
Monitoring path
The article’s click path is Monitoring → Troubleshooting. [cite:1]
21-day window
Troubleshooting shows calls for the past 21 days. Analytics can cross-launch into that same 21-day slice. [cite:1] [cite:2]
Call segment
One piece of a call with its own outcome. A transfer can be several segments. The view is built so you can see which segment failed. [cite:1]
Correlation ID
Groups related calls in the search view. Filters are not available when you searched by phone number or correlation ID. [cite:1]
Hop
A leg of the media path. The article tells you to open hop details to see where quality broke. [cite:1]
ICE
Interactive Connectivity Establishment. One way media stays directly between the users instead of crossing the Webex Calling cloud. [cite:1]
PNC
Private Network Connect. One of the path-optimization values the article lists next to ICE and “No optimization.” [cite:1]
Path optimization
The call-details field. Accepted values in the article: ICE, PNC, or No optimization. [cite:1]
Partial search
Phone number, user name, and device name can be partial. For a phone number, the first digits list candidates. Enter alone searches for an exact match. A pasted number needs the leading +1 for that search to work. [cite:1]
CSV export
Search results or troubleshooting call records, up to 100 records per file, for the tab you are on. [cite:1]
JSON call report
From the call details page, along with participant details as CSV. [cite:1]
Analytics
Historical charts. Standard customers get 3 months. Pro Pack customers get 13 months. Not for billing. [cite:2]
Selectable range
The article says that starting January 27, 2026 a Pro Pack admin can select at most 3 months at a time, while 13 months of history remain. That date is before this Canon pass. re-check the live picker. [cite:2]
Webex App calling history
A separate sentence: Pro Pack has 13 months for calls based in Webex App, and everyone else has 3 months for those calls. Do not paste that sentence onto every Calling report without reading which dashboard you opened. [cite:2]
Calling media quality
A Control Hub dashboard of KPIs and charts by location, IP, media type, connection, codec, endpoint, and phone model. [cite:2]
Worst-experience cross-launch
Clicking a name in Users with worst calling experience opens Troubleshooting for that user’s media sessions in the selected dates, capped at 21 days. [cite:2]
Logs page
Troubleshooting → Logs. Search the user by email and choose Collect logs. [cite:3]
30-day client logs
Uploaded Webex App logs are available for the last 30 days. The user can also send logs from the app. [cite:3]
Offline collection
You cannot collect logs for a user who is offline. They need an active internet connection. [cite:3]

06 · Config

Click paths, not a CLI

  1. Sign in to Control Hub. Go to Monitoring → Troubleshooting. [cite:1]
  2. Search. For a phone number you paste, keep the +1. A short prefix lists candidates. An email opens both a meetings tab and a calling tab. [cite:1]
  3. Open the segment. Read the outcome, the hops, and path optimization: ICE, PNC, or No optimization. [cite:1]
  4. Export with Export → Download search results (CSV) or Download troubleshooting call records (CSV). Stay on the Webex Calling tab if that is the file you want. The cap is 100 records. The details page can also download a JSON report and a participant CSV. [cite:1]
  5. For trends, open Analytics. Standard history is 3 months. Pro Pack history is 13 months. Select at most 3 months in one range after the change dated January 27, 2026. [cite:2]
  6. From Users with worst calling experience, click the name to land in Troubleshooting for that range, up to 21 days. [cite:2]
  7. For the app itself, go to Troubleshooting → Logs, search the email, and choose Collect logs, then Collect. The user must be online. Uploaded logs last 30 days. [cite:3]

09 · Self-question ladder

Twelve rungs

L1Where do you open Webex Calling troubleshooting?

Control Hub, Monitoring → Troubleshooting. [cite:1]

L1How far back does that view go?

21 days. [cite:1]

L2What is a call segment for?

Each segment can succeed, fail, or be refused on its own, so a transfer is not one blob. [cite:1]

L2What path-optimization values does the article list?

ICE, PNC, and No optimization. [cite:1]

L2How many troubleshooting records fit in one CSV?

100. And only the tab you are viewing. [cite:1]

L3Why did filters vanish?

The search was a phone number or a correlation ID. [cite:1]

L3What is the difference between 13 months and a 3-month picker?

Pro Pack keeps 13 months. Starting January 27, 2026 the article says you select at most 3 months at a time. [cite:2]

L3What does the worst-experience click do?

Opens Troubleshooting for that user’s media sessions in the chosen dates, up to 21 days. [cite:2]

L4How do you collect Webex App logs?

Troubleshooting → Logs, search the email, Collect logs, then Collect. [cite:3]

L4How long are uploaded logs kept, and who else can send them?

30 days. The user can send them from the app. An offline user cannot be collected. [cite:3]

L5Can you bill from Analytics?

No. The article says the data is for general use and should not be used for billing. [cite:2]

L5Which calling history sentence is only about Webex App?

Pro Pack gets 13 months, and others 3 months, for calls based in Webex App. Read the dashboard you actually opened before you repeat it. [cite:2]

13 · Interview

Oral answers

30 seconds. Monitoring → Troubleshooting is 21 days of Webex Calling media sessions: segments, hops, and ICE, PNC, or no path optimization. Analytics is the longer history. Logs are the client.

2 minutes. Search an email and you get two tabs. Export the tab you are on, 100 rows. A pasted number needs +1. Correlation IDs group calls and remove filters. Pro Pack stores 13 months and, since January 27, 2026, you select three months at a time. A standard org has three months total.

30 seconds, logs. Troubleshooting → Logs, by email, Collect. Thirty days. The user has to be online, or they send the logs themselves from the app.

2 minutes, what you will not conclude. An empty troubleshooting search can mean the SIP leg died on the Local Gateway before a media session existed. Analytics is not a bill. A *.my.webex.com site is outside this analytics article.

Draw it. Three boxes: 21 days, 3-or-13 months, 30 days. Put the click path under each.
