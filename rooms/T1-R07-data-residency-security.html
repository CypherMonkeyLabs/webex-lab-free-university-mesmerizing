00 · Header

T1 · Control Hub & Platform Foundations

Data Residency & Security Basics

The country you select while provisioning permanently sets the GEO for the organization’s users and encryption keys. Webex does not currently support migrating between GEO locations. Meetings follow the site time zone. Calling follows the principal place of business given at order time. You read the result at Account > Info > Data Locations.

Room T1-R07Version v0.1last-verified: 2026-10-14Difficulty L2Est. time 70–90 minPrereq T1-R01Reading 18 min

Key takeaways

  • Identities, encryption keys, and encrypted user-generated content are stored in the organization’s geographic region. The Key Management Service in that region handles key requests for spaces, messages, and content in Webex App. [cite:1]
  • Messaging GEOs named in the article are Europe (Frankfurt and Amsterdam, mapped to Continental Europe, the Middle East, and Africa) and North America and the rest of the world (United States data centers). [cite:1]
  • A European Union city time zone puts Meetings data in Frankfurt. Other time zones use the data center closest to the selected city. [cite:1]
  • Audit log retention is one year in most regions and three years in India. A search can include only the past year. [cite:1]
  • AI-generated content for meetings and messages is stored in the same data center as the user-generated content. [cite:1]

01 · Executive summary

Five lines, then why it matters

  • Check Meetings, Messaging, and common application data at Account > Info > Data Locations. Check Contact Center at Contact Center > Settings > Service Details > Country of Operation. [cite:1]
  • CountryCodeGEOMapping.xlsx maps a country to a region. On the User page tab, cell B3 is the country and cell B4 is the region. [cite:2]
  • Space content is stored in the region of the organization that owns the space. A 1:1 chat that includes a North America or rest-of-world user is stored in that GEO. [cite:1]
  • Compliance officers keep visibility of user content regardless of where it is stored, including eDiscovery and CASB. [cite:1]
  • Eligible legacy moves named on August 29, 2025 are European Union common application data, Saudi Arabia Webex Meetings sites, and UAE common application data. That header is older than 12 months. re-check [cite:3]
Why it matters. A design that picks the country after the org exists is already late. Calling, Meetings, and Messaging do not share one residency switch.

04 · Ecosystem

Ecosystem and sister technologies

Glossary

Data residency
Storage of user data in regional data centers that correspond to the customer’s location. Formerly called data locality. [cite:1]
GEO
The location set at provisioning for the organization’s users and encryption keys. A later move between GEOs is not supported. [cite:1]
KMS
The Key Management Service in the organization’s region. It handles requests for keys that encrypt and decrypt spaces, messages, and content in Webex App. [cite:1]
Country Selector
The Control Hub drop-down during provisioning that maps a country to a data center. [cite:1]
Data Locations
Account > Info > Data Locations, where an admin verifies Meetings, Messaging, and common application data. [cite:1]
Country of Operation
The Contact Center setting that confirms that service’s residency. [cite:1]
Space owner
The organization whose region stores the content that appears in the space. [cite:1]
iPOP
Internet Point of Presence. The Meetings article lists them separately from data centers. [cite:4]

06 · Config

Click paths

  1. During organization provisioning, use the Country Selector. Prefer the country where most users are, even if the business address differs. [cite:1]
  2. Before you commit, download CountryCodeGEOMapping.xlsx. On the User page tab, set B3 and read B4. Or browse the Country Code GEO mapping tab. [cite:2]
  3. After the org exists, sign in to Control Hub and open Account > Info > Data Locations for Meetings, Messaging, and common application data. [cite:1]
  4. For Contact Center, open Contact Center > Settings > Service Details > Country of Operation. [cite:1]
  5. When you create a Meetings site, the time zone selects the Meetings data center. An EU city time zone is Frankfurt in this article. [cite:1]
  6. For Calling, confirm the principal place of business the partner submitted at order time. The article does not give a Control Hub control that moves that choice. [cite:1]
  7. Do not plan a later move between Messaging GEOs. The article says that migration is not supported. [cite:1]
  8. If the customer is in a legacy migration program, read the data-center migration article before you promise a move. re-check [cite:3]

13 · Interview

Oral answers

30 seconds. The Country Selector permanently sets the GEO for users and encryption keys. I cannot move that GEO later. I verify Meetings, Messaging, and common application data at Account, Info, Data Locations.

2 minutes. Messaging Europe is Frankfurt and Amsterdam for Continental Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. North America and the rest of the world is in the United States. If most users are in one country, I pick that country. If they are spread out, I pick the country with the most users, and everyone is stored there. Meetings use the site time zone, and an EU city is Frankfurt. Calling uses the principal place of business from the order. Contact Center uses Country of Operation. AI content stays with the user-generated content. Audit retention is a year in most regions and three years in India, and search is only the past year.

30 seconds, spaces. The creating organization owns a group space. A team owns spaces created in it. A space moved into a team later keeps its original owner. A 1:1 that includes North America or the rest of the world is stored in that GEO.

2 minutes, what I will not merge. I will not read the Meetings data-center list as the Messaging GEO. Free Meetings accounts may sit outside the holder’s region, and that list can change. I will not promise the August 29, 2025 migration regions without a re-check. Compliance visibility continues across regions. Partners can create customer orgs in any region where the service is offered.

What you will say about a later country change. The article says the organization stays in the GEO where it was created.
