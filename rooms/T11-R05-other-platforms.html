03 · Core

What the opened pages say

The UC Lab that opened is a free study lab for SIP, CUCM, CUBE, SRST, Expressway, voice QoS, UCCE, and a careful move toward Webex Calling. [cite:21]

The Amazon Connect lab that opened teaches flows, queues, routing profiles, and bridges from Cisco UCCE, Genesys, and Webex as study notes. It is not a Cisco import tool. [cite:21]

The Microsoft Voice lab that opened names Teams Phone, Operator Connect, Direct Routing, and Dynamics 365 Contact Center. [cite:21]

The Five9 lab that opened says it is a student’s open lab and that citations stay on the page. [cite:21]

The Genesys Cloud lab that opened names Architect, bullseye routing, and BYOC, and it says the lab does not sign in to an organization. [cite:21]

The NICE CXone page that opened says Contact Path is free study material and is not affiliated with NiCE. [cite:21]

The SIP Universe lab that opened is SIP study, from the first INVITE through media. [cite:21]

The AI lab that opened is general agent study, not a Webex admin guide. [cite:21]
