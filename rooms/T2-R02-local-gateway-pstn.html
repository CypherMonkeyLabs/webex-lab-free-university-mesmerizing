T2 · Webex Calling

Local Gateway & PSTN Options

Every location picks one PSTN path. Local Gateway is the premises session border controller that keeps your carrier. It is not the registrar for phones.

Room T2-R02 Version v0.2 last-verified: TODO Difficulty L2 Est. time 100–120 min Prereq T1-R01 · T2-R01 planned Reading 34 min

Key takeaways

  • Webex Calling documents three PSTN choices, selected per location: Cisco PSTN (Cisco Calling Plan), Cloud Connected PSTN, and premises-based PSTN (Local Gateway). You may mix them across locations. Each location has one. [cite:1]
  • Local Gateway terminates a trunk on a supported session border controller. Two trunk models exist: registration-based and certificate-based. They differ in scale and device support. [cite:2] [cite:3]
  • Registration-based trunks are up to 250 concurrent calls per trunk and support only CUBE. Certificate-based trunks are for more than 250 concurrent calls per trunk and a choice of device types. [cite:3]
  • Signaling to Webex Calling uses SIP over TLS, and media uses SRTP. [cite:4] Phones do not register through the Local Gateway; Cisco Live states endpoints register to Webex Calling over the internet. [cite:5]

01 · Executive summary

Five lines, then why it matters

  • Webex Calling documents three PSTN choices, selected per location: Cisco PSTN (Cisco Calling Plan), Cloud Connected PSTN, and premises-based PSTN (Local Gateway). You may mix them across locations. Each location has one. [cite:1]
  • Local Gateway terminates a trunk on a supported session border controller. Two trunk models exist: registration-based and certificate-based. They differ in scale and device support. [cite:2] [cite:3]
  • Registration-based trunks are up to 250 concurrent calls per trunk and support only CUBE. Certificate-based trunks are for more than 250 concurrent calls per trunk and a choice of device types. [cite:3]
  • Signaling to Webex Calling uses SIP over TLS, and media uses SRTP. [cite:4] Phones do not register through the Local Gateway; Cisco Live states endpoints register to Webex Calling over the internet. [cite:5]
  • Webex for Government uses the certificate-based trunk, and the getting-started article says CUBE is the only SBC that currently supports Webex for Government. [cite:3] [cite:4]
Why it matters. PSTN choice is the first architecture decision on every calling migration. Interviewers listen for “per location, one option” and for the registration-versus-certificate split. Guessing ports or carrier lists is how this answer falls apart.

03 · Concept deep-dive

Choose the path before you touch dial peers

The calling setup article is blunt: the PSTN choice is at location level, each location has only one PSTN option, and you can mix options across a deployment. After you provision an option you can change it from Manage on the location’s PSTN properties, but some options — Cisco PSTN is the example — may not be available once another option was assigned. Open a support case for guidance rather than clicking until it works. [cite:1]

What the gateway is not

BRKCOL-2312 (Cisco Live 2025) states that endpoint registration is not proxied through the Local Gateway. Endpoints register directly to Webex Calling over the internet. The gateway’s job is premises PSTN or IP-PBX connectivity, and it can be part of a paced migration. [cite:5] Survivability Gateway is a different service: BRKCOL-2312 describes it as a site gateway managed from Control Hub that routes internal, external, and emergency calls during a network outage. Co-locating LGW and SGW has its own constraints in that deck, including an IOS-XE release note. Survivability is T2-R07; do not collapse the two names in this room.

Tip

Write the location’s PSTN choice on the design in the same sentence as the trunk type. “LGW” alone does not tell you registration versus certificate, and that choice changes the certificate plan, the firewall, and the call budget. [cite:3]

Gotcha

For registration trunks the IOS-XE guide says max-conn on the Webex dial peer should be 250 or lower. Setting it higher does not raise the documented trunk limit. [cite:4] Certificate trunks that share an IP and port across customers can lose capacity; the partner-hosted gateway article says separate TCP connections per trunk support the maximum concurrent call capacity. [cite:8]

Ports

BRKCOL-2812 (2024) told viewers to permit TCP 8934 or 5062 and UDP 19560–65535, and to follow the updated Webex Calling port reference. 04 · Ecosystem

Ecosystem and sister technologies

A Local Gateway is a trunk, not the whole calling system. Emergency calling, the carrier choice, the SBC, and call recording sit beside it and fail independently. This section names those sisters. Peer PBX and contact-center products are orientation labels. The hub ecosystem map holds the cross-vendor picture.

Glossary

Local Gateway
The premises session border controller that terminates a Webex Calling trunk. Registration-based trunks are up to 250 concurrent calls and CUBE only. Certificate-based trunks are for more than 250 and a choice of device types. [cite:3]
CUBE
Cisco’s IOS XE session border controller. It is the only device for a registration trunk, and the getting-started article says it is the only SBC that currently supports Webex for Government. [cite:3] [cite:4]
Premises PSTN
One of the three PSTN families for a location, alongside Cisco Calling Plan and Cloud Connected PSTN. The trunk is how premises PSTN reaches Webex Calling. [cite:1] [cite:7]
RedSky
The emergency provider in the enhanced emergency calling article. You get a RedSky account, set alert email addresses, and allow the phones and the Webex client to reach RedSky. [cite:14]
HELD / HELD+
HTTP Enabled Location Delivery. The article lists HELD+ as used by Webex App, with dynamic location via network discovery using a wiremap. Supported clients named there include Cisco MPP phones and Webex App. [cite:14]
Dispatchable location
RAY BAUM’S Act Phase 2, begun January 2022, requires a correct dispatch address for non-fixed devices and nomadic users, including a Webex App user who leaves the premises. [cite:14]
Kari’s Law
Public Law 115-127. A call from the organization to emergency services must generate an email notification. Control Hub can enable that for all locations or for selected locations. [cite:15] [cite:14]
933
A North American emergency test number for locations in the United States and Canada. It tests emergency-call email generation and does not alert public safety personnel. [cite:15]
ECBN
Emergency Callback Number. Extension-only users need an accurate ECBN: the location default, or another telephone number from that same location. [cite:17]
Call recording provider
The provider you select for the organization before anyone enables recording for users. Article nhvl4hw says both of these: “Webex is the default value,” and, later, “Dubber is chosen by default, as there is no additional cost to include Dubber Go.” This Canon cites both sentences. It does not claim a screenshot of which control paints first. [cite:16]
Dubber Go
Included with Webex Calling user entitlements at no additional cost. Unlimited recordings, each stored 30 days, and only the user manages them in the Dubber portal. [cite:16]
Survivability Gateway
A different service from Local Gateway. BRKCOL-2312 describes it as a site gateway, managed from Control Hub, that routes internal, external, and emergency calls during an outage. [cite:5]

09 · Recursive self-question

Ladder

L0What is a Local Gateway?

The premises SBC that terminates a Webex Calling trunk so a location can use premises-based PSTN or reach a non-cloud site. [cite:1] [cite:2]

L1Why does it exist?

Cisco PSTN and Cloud Connected PSTN replace the carrier in the cloud. Local Gateway exists when you keep your carrier, your analog, or a PBX next door. Without it, that location has no documented premises PSTN path. [cite:1]

L1aWhat breaks if I also expect phones to register to it?

You will troubleshoot the wrong box. Endpoints register to Webex Calling. The gateway is a trunk, not a proxy registrar. [cite:5]

L2How does a premises call work end to end?

Carrier ↔ LGW ↔ TLS/SRTP ↔ Webex Calling ↔ endpoint. The location’s numbers and route selection decide whether step three has anywhere to go. [cite:4] [cite:1]

L2aWhat is different on a certificate trunk?

Trust is the FQDN in the certificate, not a digest password. SIP from the LGW carries that FQDN in Contact. OPTIONS drive the online status in Control Hub. [cite:2] [cite:3]

L2bWhere do route groups sit?

In Control Hub, with trunks and dial plans. A trunk that is not selected as the location’s premises PSTN does not carry that location’s calls just because the CUBE is up. [cite:2] [cite:1]

L3How do I prove which failure I have?

Four evidence piles: Control Hub trunk status, location number list, CUBE registration or TLS/OPTIONS, and whether the endpoint is registered in Control Hub. Audio failures are a fifth pile (RTP), after signaling succeeds.

L3aHow do I prove a scale miss versus a bug?

If the trunk is registration-based, the documented ceiling is 250 concurrent calls and max-conn should not be set above 250. Crossing that is a design miss. [cite:3] [cite:4]

L3bHow do I prove a certificate identity miss?

Read the CN/SAN and the Control Hub FQDN as strings. They must match. Wildcards are unsupported. Public DNS must resolve the name. [cite:2] [cite:10] [cite:3]

L4How does this differ across Calling, WxCC, WxCCE, and Dedicated Instance?

This room is multi-tenant Webex Calling. Contact-center voice can ride Webex Calling PSTN services even without a Calling subscription for the contact-center order itself — that claim belongs to the WxCC architecture article and is taught in T3-R01. [cite:12] Dedicated Instance has its own Local Gateway patterns and, for DI-to-multi-tenant contact-center support, help says certificate-based trunks are required because registration-based trunks are no longer supported on that interop. [cite:13] WxCCE PSTN is not this trunk model; do not answer an enterprise-routing question with CUBE max-conn 250.

L4aIs Survivability Gateway just a second name for LGW?

No. BRKCOL-2312 treats SGW as the outage path for endpoints when the cloud is unreachable, managed from Control Hub, and lists co-location constraints that include provisioning the gateway as Survivability Gateway rather than as Local Gateway. Detail is T2-R07. [cite:5]

L5If I redesigned PSTN attachment, what would I change?

I would want one trunk type so labs stop splitting digest and certificates. The constraint is scale and device support: registration is the simple CUBE model at 250 calls, and certificate exists because that model does not scale. The partner-hosted article says certificate trunking was created to solve registration’s scale limits. [cite:8] [cite:3]

L5aWhat would I refuse to hard-code?

Firewall ports from a 2024 Cisco Live slide are replaced by b2exve as of 2026-10-14. The country list from the undated at-a-glance PDF stays marked in the limits table. [cite:21] against current help before a build. [cite:9] [cite:7]
