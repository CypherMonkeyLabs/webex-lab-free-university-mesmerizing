00 · Header

T2 · Webex Calling

Devices & Provisioning

A phone joins Webex Calling by MAC address or by a 16-digit activation code. A blank MAC in Control Hub generates the code. The code lasts 30 days on an MPP phone and 7 days on a Room device. A professional workspace license holds up to five phones. A common-area workspace holds one.

Room T2-R04Version v0.1last-verified: 2026-10-14Difficulty L2Est. time 70–90 minPrereq T2-R01, T1-R04Reading 18 min

Key takeaways

  • Add a device by MAC address or by activation code. A blank MAC generates the code. RoomOS uses activation codes. [cite:1]
  • The activation-code subset includes 6800, 7800, and 8800 MPP models named in the article, conference phones 7832 and 8832, Desk Phone 9800, Wireless 9821, and Video Phone 8875. [cite:1]
  • A 16-digit code expires after 30 days for MPP phones and after 7 days for Room devices. [cite:2]
  • Professional workspace: up to five Cisco desk phones, MPP phones, wireless, ATAs, Phone OS, or third-party devices. Common area: one device. Multiple devices are added from the new workspace page. [cite:3]
  • Configure an ATA’s VLAN, IP address, and subnet mask before activation. After activation those settings are not available to change. [cite:2]

01 · Executive summary

Five lines, then why it matters

  • MPP firmware 11.3.1 SR2 or later is required to enter an activation code on the phone. The phone reboots and onboards after the code is entered. [cite:1]
  • CSV device type IP is for MPP, ATA, and DECT. WEBEX or WEBEX_CALLING is for RoomOS workspaces. The article’s example MAC is 001A2B3C4D5E. [cite:1]
  • A workspace device is assigned to the workspace, not to a user. Add it from Management > Devices > Add device, or from Management > Workspaces. [cite:3]
  • Calling service choices for a Board, Desk, or Room workspace include None, Call on Webex (1:1, non-PSTN, the default), Cisco Webex Calling, premises calling, and legacy Hybrid Calling. [cite:3]
  • Only specific roles can generate activation codes. The article points at the organization-role article for which roles. Role names stay in T1-R05. [cite:2]
Why it matters. A phone without a MAC or a code never registers. The license on the workspace decides whether a second phone is even allowed.

Tip

Enter the activation code only after the MPP is on firmware 11.3.1 SR2 or later. The phone reboots as part of onboarding. [cite:1]

Gotcha

An ATA’s network settings are fixed once the device activates. Change VLAN, IP address, and subnet mask before you enter the code. [cite:2]

06 · Config

Click paths

  1. For a user phone, open Users, select the user, open Devices, and click Add Device. Choose the device type and click Add device. [cite:2]
  2. For a shared phone, open Management > Devices > Add device, or Management > Workspaces > Add workspace. Choose Shared usage, then New workspace. [cite:3]
  3. Name the workspace within 128 characters and avoid % # < > / \ and ". Set room type, capacity, and location. [cite:3]
  4. Choose Cisco Desk Phone, or Cisco MPP, Wireless, ATA, or third-party device with Cisco Managed Devices. Pick Professional workspace or Common area workspace. [cite:3]
  5. Assign the location’s phone number and, if you want one, an extension. Save. [cite:3]
  6. Enter a MAC address, or leave it blank so Control Hub generates an activation code. For RoomOS, use the code. [cite:1]
  7. Copy, email, or print the 16-digit code. On an MPP, confirm firmware 11.3.1 SR2 or later, enter the code, and let the phone reboot. [cite:1] [cite:2]
  8. For an ATA, set VLAN, IP address, and subnet mask before that activation. [cite:2]
  9. For Board, Desk, or Room Series, choose the calling service: None, Call on Webex, Cisco Webex Calling, premises calling, or legacy Hybrid Calling. Activate with the code. [cite:3]

13 · Interview

Oral answers

30 seconds. I claim a phone by MAC or by a 16-digit activation code. MPP codes last 30 days and Room codes last 7. A professional workspace holds five phones. A common-area workspace holds one.

2 minutes. All of the listed devices can use a MAC. The activation-code subset is the 6800, 7800, and 8800 models the September 2026 article names, plus 7832, 8832, 9800, 9821, and 8875. RoomOS uses a code, and the CSV types are WEBEX or WEBEX_CALLING. An MPP needs firmware 11.3.1 SR2 before the code is typed, then it reboots. I can copy, email, or print the code, including to someone who is not the assignee. An ATA gets its VLAN and IP address first, because those settings lock after activation. If the room needs PSTN I choose Cisco Webex Calling, not the default Call on Webex service.

30 seconds, delete. Deleting a workspace factory-resets an online MPP and returns it to the code screen. An offline phone needs a manual reset.

2 minutes, what I will not merge. I will not put a second device type in one space unless Companion Mode or a standalone Room Navigator applies. I will not promise PSTN on Call on Webex. I will not generate a code with a role the organization-role article does not allow. Migration licenses from Enterprise firmware are a different article.

What you will say about the nightly job. The workspace article says the location is updated during the nightly resync.
