Calling Analytics & CDR

T2-R08. Control Hub Calling analytics, the Detailed Call History tab, and why a KPI call is not the same object as a call-leg row. n0rlwxe header September 29, 2026. Retrieved 2026-10-14.

Reading 14 minv0.1last-verified: TODOL2DraftStudy notes

Key takeaways

  • Calling analytics keeps 13 months for Webex App calls with Pro Pack, and 3 months without it. [cite:1]
  • From January 27, 2026 a selection is 3 months at a time even when 13 months of history remains. [cite:1]
  • Detailed Call History KPIs count Alice calling Bob as one call. The table writes two legs when both are registered Calling users. [cite:1]
  • A media session is good only when both audio and video stay under 150 ms jitter, 400 ms latency, and 5% packet loss. [cite:1]

01 · TL;DR

One dashboard, two grains

  • The Calling analytics sentence is 13 months with Pro Pack and 3 months without, for calls based in Webex App. [cite:1]
  • The January 27, 2026 picker change limits a selection to 3 months. History access is a separate sentence. [cite:1]
  • Detailed Call History does not include the Call on Webex option. [cite:1]
  • Voicemail answers count as answered, on the KPI and on the leg. [cite:1]
  • The worst-experience name opens Troubleshooting, still capped at 21 days. [cite:1]
Why it matters. Interviewers ask for “the CDR.” This room separates the per-call KPI, the per-leg table, the call-queue charts, and the 21-day troubleshooting hop. Mixing them is how a retention number gets pasted onto the wrong page.

03 · Core

Keep the grains apart

n0rlwxe, header September 29, 2026, says you use the Analytics page in Control Hub to see historical Webex Calling data. You have access to 13 months of data for calls based in Webex App if the organization has Pro Pack. Without Pro Pack, that access is 3 months. The same article’s January 27, 2026 note says the maximum date range you can select becomes 3 months at a time, while 13 months of historical data remains available if you run more than one report. [cite:1]

A media session is categorized as good if both the video stream and the audio stream had jitter below 150 ms, latency below 400 ms, and packet loss below 5%. Clicking a name in Users with worst calling experience opens Troubleshooting for the media sessions that user made in the selected range, up to 21 days. [cite:1]

Per call versus per leg

Detailed Call History KPIs are generated on a per call basis. Alice calling Bob counts as a single call. The table entries are individual call legs, and each Webex Calling call is comprised of an originating record and a terminating record when both people are registered Webex Calling users. If Alice is a PSTN user, Webex Calling logs only the terminating entry for Bob. [cite:1]

Two handle-time sentences

On the call-queue stats table, handle time includes talk time, hold time, and ring time. On the call-queue agents table, handle minutes are calculated as total talk time plus total hold time. Those sentences are both in n0rlwxe. Do not collapse them into one formula. [cite:1]

The Direction filter works only when Call classification is set to External. The detailed call history tab does not include the Call on Webex option. Consultative transfer, call park, and call retrieve are counted as an additional internal call because those CDRs have a different correlation id. The Country field populates for international calls with the country code of the caller ID, and the article says that field may appear empty for some admins. [cite:1]

Call-queue incoming KPIs on that page are total answered calls, total abandoned calls, percentage of abandoned calls, and average wait time. Abandoned means the caller hung up or left a message before an agent became available. Overflow - Busy is the queue limit. Overflow - Timed out is the wait past the maximum configured limit. Live queue KPIs are active calls, calls waiting, and held calls. [cite:1]

Partner Hub article n5zr85e says multi-tenant partners can set a webhook to gather Webex Calling records for customers they manage, and that the webhook delivers detailed call history records every 5 minutes. Header August 13, 2026. [cite:2]

Ecosystem and sister technologies
