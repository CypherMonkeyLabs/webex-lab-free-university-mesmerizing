T3 · Webex Contact Center (Multi-tenant)

WxCC Multi-tenant Overview

This track is the cloud contact center Cisco’s help center calls Webex Contact Center: a CCaaS platform, not the Enterprise product that inherits UCCE. The Canon nickname “multi-tenant” follows Cisco Live portfolio language. Confirm it against the contract before you say it to a customer.

Room T3-R01 Version v0.2 last-verified: TODO Difficulty L2 Est. time 100–120 min Prereq T1-R01 Reading 38 min

Key takeaways

  • Cisco’s architecture article calls Webex Contact Center a contact center as a service, built cloud-native: independent services, event-driven messaging, stateless services on Kubernetes, deployed in AWS. [cite:1]
  • Cisco Live decks label that cloud product “Webex Contact Center (Multi-tenant Cloud)” and the other product “Webex Contact Center Enterprise (Dedicated Instance Cloud).” [cite:2] [cite:3] re-check
  • Voice lands on an entry point (a phone number). A flow runs. A queue holds the contact. Teams at sites are the agent search space. [cite:1] [cite:4]
  • Digital channels enter through Webex Connect. Control Hub is the onboarding console. Analyzer is the reporting surface fed by the event pipeline. [cite:1]

01 · Executive summary

Five lines, then why it matters

  • Cisco’s architecture article calls Webex Contact Center a contact center as a service, built cloud-native: independent services, event-driven messaging, stateless services on Kubernetes, deployed in AWS. [cite:1]
  • Cisco Live decks label that cloud product “Webex Contact Center (Multi-tenant Cloud)” and the other product “Webex Contact Center Enterprise (Dedicated Instance Cloud).” [cite:2] [cite:3] re-check
  • Voice lands on an entry point (a phone number). A flow runs. A queue holds the contact. Teams at sites are the agent search space. [cite:1] [cite:4]
  • Digital channels enter through Webex Connect. Control Hub is the onboarding console. Analyzer is the reporting surface fed by the event pipeline. [cite:1]
  • New Webex Contact Center subscriptions include Webex Calling PSTN services, and the architecture article says calls can be routed to the contact center without a separate Webex Calling subscription. [cite:1]
Why it matters. People say “Webex contact center” and mean three different stacks: this cloud tenant, Webex Contact Center Enterprise, or on-prem UCCE/PCCE. This room is the fence. Interview answers that mention Router, Logger, or CVP belong in T4, not here.

03 · Concept deep-dive

Name the product before you name the boxes

Help.webex’s architecture article does not use the phrase “multi-tenant” in the sections read for this room. It defines Webex Contact Center as CCaaS, cloud native, on AWS, with services in Kubernetes across availability zones. [cite:1] Cisco Live PSOCCT-1014 (2024) and BRKCCT-2031 (2025) do use “Webex Contact Center (Multi-tenant Cloud)” opposite “Webex Contact Center Enterprise (Dedicated Instance Cloud).” [cite:2] [cite:3] This Canon track follows that portfolio split. Agent-count slogans on those slides are not copied into the limits table: the extracted text does not prove which number sits on which product. The older Setup and Administration Guide uses the same four words — sites, teams, entry points, queues — and adds a useful constraint: an agent may be assigned to more than one team but services only one team at a time. It also says only globalized +E.164 PSTN numbers map to entry points; extensions are not supported for that mapping. [cite:4] That guide opens by saying it is no longer updated because many portal entities moved to Control Hub. Treat those two sentences as still useful only after you re-check them in a current help article. They are included because no newer article in this pass contradicted them, and they are easy to get wrong.

What the flow is allowed to do

The architecture article’s representative activities: play messages, collect user data, transfer, send to a virtual agent, and queue. It also names QueueToAgent (voice, direct to an agent id, with an agent-specific queue if that agent is busy), GetQueueInfo (position in queue, estimated wait time, agents available), Callback (caller disconnects and keeps place), and overflow using capacity-based teams with an external DN, usually as the last distribution cycle. [cite:1] The routing article adds the queue as a holding area for telephony, chat, email, or social, and names skill-based and non-skill-based queues. Its overview names Longest Available, Circular, and Best Available. The architecture article’s agent-selection section names Longest Available Agent routing and, under skill-based routing, LAA or BAA. [cite:6] [cite:1] The routing article’s agent-assignment section also names Linear/Top-down. That is the fourth name, and it is not offered on every queue type. T3-R03 keeps the four names in their lanes. Open the queue in Control Hub and read the patterns that queue type offers. A fifth name is not in these articles, and the list changes when Cisco adds a pattern.

Access control is not “all supervisors see all queues”

Administrators can build user profiles scoped to sites, teams, queues, and entry points. Sites and teams are hierarchical: site access narrows which teams you see. Queues and entry points are global at organization level, so a multi-country design creates separate entry points and queues and then grants them out. [cite:1]

Tip

When you draw a contact, write the entry point and the flow on the arrow into the queue. A queue with no flow in front of it is a Calling-queue habit. On this platform the flow decides to queue. [cite:1]

Gotcha

The Setup and Administration Guide still describes a Management Portal with Provisioning, Routing Strategy, and Call Monitoring modules, and it tells you the guide is no longer updated. Do not teach a new admin to live in that portal until you confirm the task has not moved to Control Hub or Flow Designer. Queue create in a current article is Contact Center navigation → Customer experience → Queues. [cite:4] [cite:8]

Enterprise is a different room

PSOCCT-1014 describes the Enterprise cloud as multi-instance application services, high-end scale, highly customizable, a differentiated admin experience, and low disruption for Unified CCE and Packaged CCE customers. None of that is configured with the entry-point/flow/queue objects above. [cite:2]

04 · Ecosystem

Ecosystem and sister technologies

Multi-tenant Webex Contact Center is the cloud platform: entry point, flow, queue, team, site, onboarding in Control Hub, and Analyzer fed by events. [cite:1] The sisters below are the systems that sit on that platform. Microsoft’s product Dynamics 365 Contact Center is a different name from Cisco’s CRM connector for Dynamics 365. Peer columns are orientation. The hub ecosystem map is the cross-lab picture.

Glossary

Entry point
Where a contact enters. The architecture article pairs it with a flow. [cite:1]
Flow
The routing logic a contact runs before a queue. Virtual Agent activities live in the flow. [cite:1] [cite:15]
Queue
The wait point that offers work to a team. Routing patterns are configured under Contact Center → Customer experience → Queues. [cite:8] [cite:6]
Analyzer
Reporting built from contact-center events, as the architecture article describes it. Session behavior is tajemk, retrieved 2026-10-14, not this architecture sentence. [cite:28] [cite:1]
Search API
Historical search. The developer page says the FROM parameter cannot be older than 36 months from the current time, and the duration between TO and FROM must not be more than 365 days. Scopes cjp:config or cjp:config_read. Roles: Administrator or Supervisor. [cite:9]
CRM connector
Cisco’s integration that launches the agent Desktop inside Salesforce, Dynamics 365, or ServiceNow. Digital channels such as chat and email are not supported on these connectors. [cite:12]
CIF
Microsoft’s Channel Integration Framework, as Cisco’s Dynamics article uses it. Single-session Dynamics apps require CIF version 1. Multi-session apps require CIF version 2. On-premises Dynamics is not supported. [cite:13]
Virtual Agent–Voice
Self-service that integrates the IVR with cloud AI through a Contact Center AI connector. Supported integrations named in the article: Google Dialogflow CX, Dialogflow ES, and bring-your-own virtual agent. [cite:15]
AGENT_TRANSFER
A mandatory bring-your-own virtual agent event. The call must be escalated to a queue of human agents with that event. [cite:19]
AI Assistant
A Control Hub add-on for Webex Contact Center. The enablement article includes AI-generated summaries (call drop and agent transfer), automated wellness breaks, Auto CSAT, real-time transcription, and Real-Time Assist. [cite:16]
WFO Agent Assist
Described on the agent what’s-new page as a GenAI assistant in Webex WFO for schedules, time off, and overtime, with responses in 59 languages checked against WFM rules. [cite:17]
Connect
The digital-channel path the architecture article names. Chat and email on that path are not the same thing as the CRM connectors, which exclude those channels. [cite:1] [cite:12]

09 · Recursive self-question

Ladder

L0What is Webex Contact Center in this track?

Cisco’s cloud CCaaS contact center: entry points, flows, queues, teams, and Agent Desktop, provisioned from Control Hub. The Canon calls it multi-tenant because Cisco Live does. [cite:1] [cite:2]

L1Why is it a separate product from Webex Calling queues?

Calling queues are a calling feature. This platform answers the call in a flow, queues across sites, mixes digital channels through Connect, and builds Analyzer datasets from service events. [cite:1] A side-by-side of queue math is T7-R06, not a guess in this answer.

L1aWhat breaks if I design it as “UCCE in the cloud”?

You will look for Router, Logger, and CVP. Those names belong to the Enterprise / on-prem heritage track. This article’s nouns are entry point, flow, queue, team, site. [cite:1] [cite:2]

L2How does a voice contact move?

Number → entry point → flow instance → queue → team → the phone number the agent registered at login. Events branch off to Analyzer. [cite:1]

L2aWhat does the flow do if no agent is free?

The contact parks on the queue and the treatment attached to the queueing activity continues until an agent is offered the contact and the handler is interrupted. [cite:1]

L2bWhere does chat enter?

Webex Connect, not the PSTN edge. Connect can bot-treat the thread and must explicitly queue it into Webex Contact Center. [cite:1]

L3How do I prove a routing failure?

Check entry-point match, flow reached Queue Contact, team is in the active distribution group, agent skills if the queue is skill-based, and the login station is dialable. Profile scope explains “I cannot see it,” which is not the same as “it did not route.”

L3aHow do I prove the login station?

Call the number the agent typed, from outside the queue. The article says an invalid number means the agent cannot receive incoming calls. [cite:1]

L3bHow do I prove I am in the wrong admin UI?

If the task is “create a queue” and you are in a legacy portal module the setup guide itself retired, switch to Contact Center → Customer experience → Queues and re-check. [cite:8] [cite:4]

L4How does this differ across Calling, WxCC, WxCCE, and Dedicated Instance?

Calling: location PSTN and calling features (T2). WxCC (this room): shared cloud CCaaS, flow and queue. WxCCE: Cisco Live’s dedicated-instance contact-center cloud for complex and CCE-heritage customers. [cite:2] Dedicated Instance for calling is a different UC-app deployment (T5); it can integrate with this contact center, and DI-to-multi-tenant contact-center support requires certificate-based trunks. [cite:11]

L4aDo I need a Webex Calling subscription to land a PSTN call on WxCC?

The architecture article says calls route in using Webex Calling PSTN services and that this allows routing to the contact center without a Webex Calling subscription. It also says all new Webex Contact Center subscriptions include those PSTN services automatically. [cite:1] Read the PSTN choice on the subscription and on the location in Control Hub. The architecture article says new subscriptions can include Webex Calling PSTN, and the SKU follows the order.

L5If I redesigned the object model, what would I change?

I would want queues to be site-scoped so a supervisor grant could not forget a global queue. The article’s constraint is that queues and entry points are global at organization level; site scoping is done with user profiles and by creating separate queues per geography. [cite:1]

L5aWhat would I not copy into a sizing spreadsheet?

The 300 / 6000 / 10500 figures from the setup guide, and the 400 / 48,000 phrases from the 2025 deck, until each is verified against a current limit document. [cite:4] [cite:3]
