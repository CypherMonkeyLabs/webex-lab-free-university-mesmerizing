Tenant Provisioning & Setup

T3-R02. The Services setup wizard from a trial or subscription order through country of operation, Common Edge, and the handoff to voice and digital setup. nee1mb6 header February 28, 2025. Retrieved 2026-10-14.

Key takeaways

  • The trial wizard and the subscription wizard use the same Services setup experience. [cite:1]
  • Country of operation cannot be changed after you confirm it. The tenant’s data center follows that country. [cite:1]
  • The telephony step shows Common Edge, a shared edge between Webex Calling and Webex Contact Center. [cite:1]
  • After the wizard, the article points at entities, voice channels, and digital channels. [cite:1]

01 · TL;DR

The country click is the one you cannot undo

  • Partners should finish setup before they hand the account to a customer administrator. [cite:1]
  • The customer path starts from the Welcome email’s Get Started link, then a password, then Terms of Service. [cite:1]
  • The wizard at https://admin.webex.com/ keeps appearing until setup completes. [cite:1]
  • You cannot change the country of operation after confirm. [cite:1]
  • The header on nee1mb6 is February 28, 2025. re-check [cite:1]
Why it matters. A tenant built in the wrong country is not a setting you edit later. This room is the order-to-tenant path interviews skip when they jump straight to queues.

03 · Core

Two entry doors, one wizard

nee1mb6, header February 28, 2025, says a partner or a customer administrator can set up services for trials and subscription orders, and the Services setup wizard experience is the same for both. Cisco recommends that partners complete the steps before handing the account to customer administrators. re-check [cite:1]

For a trial, the final Start New Trial screen asks whether to set up services. For a subscription, the Order Set-Up wizard ends with “Thank you. The order has been mapped correctly,” and Next continues setup. The customer administrator gets a Welcome email, opens Get Started, sets a password, chooses Save & Sign In, and accepts the Terms of Service. [cite:1]

Country does not move

On Contact Center set up, you choose the country of operation. You cannot change it after you click confirm. By default it matches the country chosen in the Order Mapping wizard. Webex Contact Center creates the tenant in the data center for that country. The country-to-data-center list is the Data Locality article. If an incompatible location message appears after Save, contact Cisco Solution Assurance. [cite:1]

SIP address is the editable one

On Calling Set up, review the SIP address name and click Next. You may modify the SIP address. You may also configure Webex Calling for the organization. Those are not the country lock. [cite:1]

The telephony section shows the connection using Common Edge services, which the article calls a shared edge infrastructure between Webex Calling and Webex Contact Center. Click Next. On Review, Submit, then Download Order Summary, then Close. The Contact Center service is then available. The follow-up click path in the article is Contact Center > Settings > Set up Cisco Webex Contact Center. [cite:1]

What to do next, in the article’s words: set up entities, set up voice options with the voice-channels article, and set up digital channels with the digital-channels article. A later block in the same article says the Data Locality list also applies for On Premises and Webex Contact Center Enterprise, and that a premise-based deployment should select a country of operation which provides services closest to the deployment. That block also says to have an order for Webex Connect or Google Contact Center AI hybrid service before you begin. [cite:1]
