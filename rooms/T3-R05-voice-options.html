00 · Header

Voice Options

T3-R05. PSTN choices and agent voice paths for Webex Contact Center: Contact Center PSTN, Cloud Connected PSTN, Local Gateway, Cisco Calling Plan, Webex Calling endpoints, and WebRTC. Article 2dputx. Retrieved 2026-10-14.

Reading 16 minv0.1last-verified: TODOL3DraftStudy notes

Key takeaways

  • The tenant needs at least one PSTN connection and at least one way to deliver calls to agents. [cite:1]
  • New subscriptions include Webex Calling PSTN services for Common Edge. A tenant from before October 2024 may need a partner to add that service. [cite:1]
  • Webex Contact Center PSTN is contiguous United States and Canada only, and it does not support international dialing. [cite:1]
  • WebRTC goes to the browser and does not use a local gateway. The article says it is unavailable for the Japan data center. [cite:1]

Prereq rooms: T3-R02 finishes the tenant. T2-R02 is the Local Gateway room. Do not paste Calling-only market counts over this article.

01 · TL;DR

PSTN is one choice. The agent path is another.

  • Cloud PSTN options in the article: Webex Contact Center PSTN, Cloud Connected PSTN, and Cisco Calling Plan for trials and supported service-number regions. [cite:1]
  • On-premises PSTN is Local Gateway, over the internet or Webex Edge Connect. [cite:1]
  • Agent methods include PSTN, Webex Calling devices, WebRTC, premise PBX, and Microsoft Teams through Local Gateway. [cite:1]
  • Login destinations are Dial Number, Extension, or Desktop. [cite:1]
  • 2dputx header June 25, 2026. [cite:1]
Why it matters. Boards fail this question by naming one PSTN product and forgetting the agent still needs a path. WebRTC, a Calling phone, and a CUCM phone are not the same hop.

03 · Core

Read the option you actually bought

Article 2dputx says a Webex Contact Center tenant must be connected to the PSTN, and agents need voice connectivity that matches their endpoints. The article recommends integrating telephony through the Webex cloud and Control Hub. If you already have a Webex Calling subscription, that subscription is used to connect voice services into Webex Contact Center as well. re-check [cite:1]

Common Edge is the shared edge. Calls are routed into Webex Contact Center using Webex Calling PSTN services, and the article says this allows routing without a Webex Calling subscription. All new Webex Contact Center subscriptions include Webex Calling PSTN services automatically. If the tenant was provisioned before October 2024, a partner may need to add Webex Calling PSTN service to use Common Edge. [cite:1]

Contact Center PSTN is a geography

Webex Contact Center PSTN is for the contiguous United States and Canada. It does not support international dialing. Calls can be delivered to and from North American Numbering Plan destinations in the contiguous lower 48 states or Canada only. An administrator creates a location and selects Webex Contact Center PSTN as the PSTN service. [cite:1]

Calling Plan is not the default PSTN

Cisco Calling Plan, in this article, is currently available for Webex Contact Center trials and in the regions where Cisco supports service and/or inbound toll-free numbers. Cloud Connected PSTN and Local Gateway are the other named production options, with the geographies the article states. [cite:1]

Agent connection methods in the article: Webex PSTN DID or cellular; Webex Calling MPP devices or Webex App, which need a Webex Calling subscription; WebRTC desktops, which deliver media to the browser and do not require a local gateway, and which are unavailable for customers hosted in the Japan data center; premise PBX agents such as Unified CM or premise PSTN; Microsoft Teams clients through Local Gateway Direct SIP Routing. The tenant needs at least one of these delivery methods. [cite:1]

At login the agent chooses Dial Number, Extension, or Desktop. A dial number is typically +E.164 and that format is not mandatory, and the number must be reachable through Webex PSTN and routing. An extension is matched to a registered Webex Calling endpoint, and a match unlocks preferred-device selection. Desktop is WebRTC, and the article repeats that it is not available for the Japan data center. [cite:1]

When Webex Calling and Webex Contact Center are deployed together, the article says to fully configure Calling, including Control Hub, licensing, PSTN, and agent endpoints such as MPP phones and Webex App, before you configure the Contact Center tenant. [cite:1]

Article gsmsaeb says that after users are synchronized you add dial numbers under Tenant Settings > Voice. For Webex Calling, the location main number does not appear in the available-number list. A toll-free entry-point mapping needs the entitlement; without Cisco Bundle 2 inbound toll-free access, and when telephony type is Cisco PSTN, use a toll number. The same article says voice settings include short call threshold, sudden disconnect threshold, default ANI, call recording, and WebRTC. Numeric threshold values were not in the extract used here. Cross-platform bridges

Unified CM is a premise agent PBX in 2dputx, not the multi-tenant call controller. Dedicated Instance agent phones are a different article (nmhk7ve) and are not redesigned here. [cite:1]

Amazon Connect and Genesys have no PSTN mapping on 2dputx.

06 · Config

Paths the articles name

  1. Finish the tenant with the Get Started article before this article’s steps. [cite:1]
  2. If Calling and Contact Center deploy together, finish Calling, licensing, PSTN, and endpoints first. [cite:1]
  3. Create a location and select the PSTN service that matches the geography you can defend from the article. [cite:1]
  4. Give the tenant at least one agent delivery method. [cite:1]
  5. Desktop profile controls which login destinations an agent sees. The article says administrators manage that behavior with desktop profiles. [cite:1]
  6. Map numbers at Tenant Settings > Voice. Do not expect the Webex Calling location main number in the list. [cite:2]

13 · Interview

Oral-ready

1. 30 seconds. The tenant needs one PSTN connection and one way to reach agents. Those are different choices. [cite:1]

1. 2 minutes. PSTN is Contact Center PSTN, Cloud Connected PSTN, Local Gateway, or Cisco Calling Plan inside the article’s limits. Agents can be PSTN, Webex Calling, WebRTC, premise, or Teams through Local Gateway. [cite:1]

2. 30 seconds. Contact Center PSTN is contiguous US and Canada and does not dial internationally. [cite:1]

2. 2 minutes. CCP is the “over 65 countries” sentence. Local Gateway is the “over 140 markets” sentence. Both sentences are on 2dputx, header June 25, 2026. [cite:1]

3. 30 seconds. WebRTC does not need a local gateway and is unavailable in the Japan data center. [cite:1]

3. 2 minutes. Desktop at login is that WebRTC option. An extension match against a registered Calling endpoint unlocks preferred device. [cite:1]

4. 30 seconds. Configure Calling before the Contact Center tenant when both are in the design. [cite:1]

4. 2 minutes. New subscriptions include Webex Calling PSTN service for Common Edge. A pre-October 2024 tenant may need the partner to add it. [cite:1]

5. 30 seconds. The location main number is not in the Webex Calling entry-point list. [cite:2]

5. 2 minutes. Toll-free mapping without Bundle 2, on Cisco PSTN, means use a toll number. I do not invent the short-call threshold digits. [cite:2]
