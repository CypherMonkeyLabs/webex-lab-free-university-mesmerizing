00 · Header

Digital Channels

T3-R06. Facebook Messenger, Livechat, Email, SMS, Apple Messages for Business, and WhatsApp through Webex Connect and Webex Engage. Article n954r0k, header August 19, 2026. Retrieved 2026-10-14.

Reading 16 minv0.1last-verified: TODOL3DraftStudy notes

Key takeaways

  • The named channels are Facebook Messenger, Livechat, Email, SMS, Apple Messages for Business, and WhatsApp. [cite:1]
  • Digital channels are controlled GA. Onboarding goes through Cisco Solution Assurance. [cite:1]
  • Email assets are created in Control Hub. SMS, Livechat, Facebook Messenger, and WhatsApp assets are created in Webex Connect and registered in Contact Center. [cite:1]
  • A skill profile is assigned to an agent. The article says a skill profile assigned to a team is not supported. [cite:1]

Prereq rooms: T3-R02 for the tenant. T8-R03 for the Connect API edge. This room is the channel design.

01 · TL;DR

Six channels, two consoles, one ordering rule

  • Connect and Engage are provisioned automatically. A new administrator must open Webex Connect within 7 days. [cite:1]
  • Authorize the Webex CC Task and Webex CC Engage nodes before you build channels. [cite:1]
  • Partner administrators do not have access to Webex Engage. [cite:1]
  • WhatsApp has a printed limitation list, including no voice messages and no read receipts on the customer side. [cite:1]
  • Inbound and outbound attachments are malware-scanned. An infected file becomes Unknown and cannot be downloaded. [cite:1]
Why it matters. Digital design fails when email is built in Connect like a chat asset, or when a team is given a skill profile the article says is unsupported.

03 · Core

Controlled availability, then a fixed sequence

n954r0k, header August 19, 2026, says Webex Contact Center supports Facebook Messenger, Livechat, Email, SMS, Apple Messages for Business, and WhatsApp. Digital channels are released in controlled GA. Only customers who have worked with Cisco Solution Assurance to plan onboarding can use them. [cite:1]

Webex Contact Center automatically provisions Webex Engage and Webex Connect, and synchronizes administrator user management. If you add a customer administrator, that person must create the user account through the Cisco email first. After administrators are added, they must create their user account and go to Webex Connect within 7 days, or recreate the account. Partner administrators do not have access to Webex Engage. [cite:1]

Role maps are three tables

External partner full administrators map to Connect Full Access. Partner provisioning admins map to Connect Limited Access. Partner read-only maps to Connect Read-only. A customer full administrator and a customer Contact Center administrator both map to Connect Tenant Owner. Customer read-only maps to Connect Read-only. The reverse table sends Connect Tenant Owner and Connect Full Access to customer full administrator, and Connect Read-only, Limited, and Restricted Access to customer read-only. [cite:1]

Email is the Control Hub asset

Create SMS, Livechat, Facebook Messenger, and WhatsApp assets in Webex Connect and register them in Webex Contact Center. Create the email asset in Control Hub. After an email asset is created, the assigned service and channel cannot be changed. [cite:1]

The click path is Control Hub > Tenant Settings > Digital, to view and launch Engage and Connect. Set the Webex CC Task and Webex CC Engage nodes to Authorized before channel setup. Then, in the Management Portal: multimedia profiles, a site that carries the profile, a team that carries the site, users as agents on that team, site, and profile, then RONA timeout. Entry points use Channel Type Social Channel, a Social Channel Type, and the asset. The setup section says to create the queue with channel type Social Channel. The skills section later says the queue channel type can be Social Channel, Email, or Chat. Quote the section you are in. [cite:1]

Engage templates control how the channel appears on Agent Desktop, including attachment types. Connect Flow Builder holds the contact lifecycle. The article also points at predefined flow templates on Cisco DevNet and GitHub. Only users with Webex Connect credentials for the registered organization can migrate flows. [cite:1]

WhatsApp limits printed in the article include: no multiple text formatting, customer multimedia arrives as attachments, Delete for Everyone does not delete the Agent Desktop copy, no voice messages, no customer-side read receipts, a blocked customer receives nothing further, a reply arrives as a standalone message, a customer cannot add the business number to a group, live location and contacts do not render, no WhatsApp payments, one service maps to one asset, and templates are not supported even if the compose box shows a template option. [cite:1]

Proficiency skills range from 0 to 10. Boolean, text, and enum are the other types. Text must match exactly. A skill profile is a set of skills assigned to an agent. The article says Webex Contact Center does not currently support a skill profile assigned to a team. Skills-based routing uses Queue Routing Type Skills Based, and the QueueTask node in Connect carries skill requirements, relaxations, and Contact Priority. [cite:1]

Article nrelvg3 says that for the Webex Contact Center integration with Webex Connect, Gmail and Microsoft 365 are the supported email providers. Header September 02, 2026.
