00 · Header

T4 · Webex Contact Center Enterprise

Reporting with CUIC

Cisco Unified Intelligence Center is the reporting platform named in the Unified CCE reporting guide. Stock bundles, Live Data, and Analyzer are three different clocks.

Room T4-R07 Version v0.1 last-verified: TODO Difficulty L2 Est. time 70–90 min Prereq T4-R01 Reading 18 min

Key takeaways

  • CUIC is a web application for Historical, Real-time, and Live Data reporting and dashboards. [cite:1] re-check
  • Live Data templates refresh much faster than Realtime or Historical reports, usually less than every 3 seconds. [cite:1] re-check
  • Stock reports are downloaded from Cisco.com and imported. An install may contain all of the bundles or a subset. [cite:1]
  • Supported screen resolution in that chapter is 1366×768 or higher. The chapter says CUIC adopts WCAG 2.1 Level A and Level AA. [cite:1]

01 · Executive summary

Five lines, then why it matters

  • Transitional templates are the introductory stock set. All Fields templates expose database fields and are the base for custom reports, including precision-queue data. [cite:1]
  • Import Outbound templates only when the deployment has Outbound Option. Import Customer Collaboration Platform templates only when that platform is deployed. [cite:1]
  • Contact Sharing templates show configuration, calls routed to each target, and calls that error during routing. Admin Security templates cover audit trails, permissions, and template ownership. [cite:1]
  • Stock report templates display data saved in the Unified CCE database. By default, stock reports have one grid view. Some also have a chart view. [cite:1] [cite:2]
  • Multi-tenant Analyzer is a different product surface. Its clocks are taught in T3-R07, not pasted onto CUIC. [cite:3]
Why it matters. A supervisor who asks for “real time” may mean Live Data, a Realtime stock template, or Analyzer. The reporting guide separates the first two. The architecture article for multi-tenant Webex Contact Center is the third.

03 · Core concepts

Bundles, views, and the clock

Unified Intelligence Center

A reporting platform for Cisco Contact Center products. The reporting user guide calls it a web-based application that provides Historical, Real-time, and Live Data reporting and dashboards. [cite:1] re-check

Stock versus custom

Cisco provides stock templates. You import them and can clone with Save As. If stock templates do not meet the need, the chapter points you at the Report Customization Guide. [cite:1] [cite:2]

Do not mix the clocks

Live Data uses the Live Data stream. The guide says its refresh is usually less than every 3 seconds and is much faster than Realtime or Historical reports. Analyzer’s real-time and historical behavior is documented on a different help article and belongs in T3-R07. [cite:1] [cite:3]

Version skew

A Cisco support note for CUIC 11.6 says mismatched stock reports after upgrades can report incorrect data because schemas change, and that the current UCCE version’s templates should be imported. That note is old. Treat the procedure as a warning to match bundles to the release, and re-check it before you run the SQL it prints. [cite:4] re-check

06 · Config

Import rules you can cite

  1. Download the stock bundles from the Intelligence Center Reports link the reporting guide names. [cite:1]
  2. Import the bundles. Expect the server to already contain all of them or only a subset, depending on the deployment. [cite:1]
  3. Import Outbound templates when Outbound Option is deployed. Import Customer Collaboration Platform templates when that platform is deployed. [cite:1]
  4. Use Transitional templates for a new reader. Use All Fields when you need every database field or precision-queue routing data. [cite:1]
  5. For a faster agent or queue wallboard, open a Live Data template: Agent, Agent Skill Group, Precision Queue, Skill Group, Recent State History, or Recent Call History. [cite:1]
  6. Clone with Save As before you change a stock report. The default view is a grid. [cite:2]
  7. Use a display at 1366×768 or higher. The chapter also states WCAG 2.1 Level A and Level AA. [cite:1]

A web-based reporting platform with Historical, Real-time, and Live Data reporting and dashboards. [cite:1]

Transitional templates are simplified introductions. All Fields templates expose database fields and include precision-queue routing data. [cite:1]

13 · Interview

Oral answers

30 seconds. CUIC is the Unified CCE reporting application. I keep Historical, Realtime, and Live Data apart. Live Data is the one the guide says usually refreshes in less than 3 seconds.

2 minutes. Stock bundles come from Cisco.com and are imported. Transitional templates are the simple set. All Fields templates are the ones I clone when I need every column, including precision queues. Outbound and Customer Collaboration Platform bundles are conditional. Contact Sharing shows targets and routing errors. Admin Security shows audit and permissions. Every stock report has a grid. I use Save As before I edit. The screen requirement printed in that chapter is 1366 by 768 or higher.

30 seconds, the other product. Analyzer is multi-tenant Webex Contact Center. I do not quote its refresh as a CUIC fact.

2 minutes, dates. The reporting chapter I used was updated April 30, 2025, so the refresh and resolution sentences stay re-check. A February 2022 what’s-new line says Webex CCE CUIC lets non-admin owners see the All Users group. An 11.6 support note warns that old stock reports lie after a schema change. I import the current bundle and I do not paste that note’s SQL into a runbook.

What you will not say. You will not invent a custom SQL report in the interview. The customization guide is the next book.
