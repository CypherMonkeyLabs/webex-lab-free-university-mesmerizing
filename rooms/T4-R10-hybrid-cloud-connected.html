00 · Header

T4 · Webex Contact Center Enterprise

Hybrid & Cloud-connected Features

Webex Contact Center Enterprise’s what’s-new page names two hybrid models, then points their internals at a partner guide. Dated feature lines stay on the month Cisco printed them.

Room T4-R10 Version v0.1 last-verified: TODO Difficulty L3 Est. time 80–100 min Prereq T4-R01 Reading 20 min

Key takeaways

  • March 2022 names two hybrid models: SBC Peering Hybrid Model and Localized RTP Hybrid Model. The internals are deferred to the partner technical guide and a white paper. [cite:1] re-check
  • July 2023 Hybrid IVR keeps some nodes on-premises, including an OTP example, and other nodes in Dialogflow CX. [cite:1]
  • July 2024 regional media keeps customer and agent media local to the region. The example says only control signals go to a U.S. tenant home. [cite:1]
  • January 2025 states 48,000 concurrent agents and 300 calls per second on a single tenant, and an early-access Edge Connect paragraph whose next sentence mentions Virtual Connect. The current Edge Connect article is a different page. [cite:1] [cite:2]

01 · Executive summary

Five lines, then why it matters

  • Equinix connectivity in the March 2022 note is a WAN to the Business Partner Network through ECX, in the U.S. and EMEA, with bandwidth from 200 MB to 10 GB. [cite:1] re-check
  • VPN-less Finesse, same month, does not support FIPPA, and does not support multiple devices reaching the desktop through NAT. [cite:1]
  • July 2023 lists email, chat, and SMS through Webex Connect, on request. January 2025 adds WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger to Email, Live Chat, and SMS. A later block on the same article also names Apple Messages for Business. Read the date on the paragraph you quote. [cite:1]
  • Windows 11 64-bit is listed for the Finesse desktop, Call Studio, and Reporting. FedRAMP is named for Webex CCE for Government. [cite:1]
  • September 2022 states Kari’s Law and RAY BAUM’s Act compliance for 911 in the U.S., and points at the partner technical guide. It also names Cloud-Connected UC analytics and CSA STAR Level 1. [cite:1]
Why it matters. Hybrid, regional media, and Edge Connect are three different sentences on one long page. Mixing the March 2022 model names with the September 2026 Edge Connect definition produces a design that neither page describes.

03 · Core concepts

Names, then the page that owns the detail

Two hybrid model names

March 2022 says Webex CCE supports the SBC Peering Hybrid Model and the Localized RTP Hybrid Model, so some contact-center and call-control appliances can stay in a premises data center while others move to the Webex CCE cloud. Further detail is the Customer Premise PSTN section of the Webex Contact Center Enterprise Partner Technical Guide and the Webex CCE Partner Managed Premise Business White Paper. Those internals are not copied here. [cite:1] re-check

Hybrid IVR is a 2023 sentence

You can keep traditional ASR, TTS, and CRM integrations and use cloud Dialogflow CX. Some nodes stay on-premises and some run in the cloud. The example is OTP generation on-premises and other tasks in the cloud. A CX bot can play a static message for up to 30 seconds while a webhook is still processing. [cite:1]

Early-access peering wording

The January 2025 early-access paragraph says Edge Connect is a managed QoS peering over Equinix Cloud Exchange or Megaport, and the next sentence says the connection insulates operations from the Internet by establishing a private circuit through Virtual Connect via VPN. That tension is in the article. The September 17, 2026 Edge Connect article defines Edge Connect as private peering that bypasses the public Internet and reaches Webex Meetings, Webex Calling, and Webex Contact Center Enterprise data centers, through Equinix or Megaport. Quote the page you are using. [cite:1] [cite:2]

48,000 is a tenant sentence

January 2025 says Webex CCE supports 48,000 concurrent agents and 300 calls per second on a single tenant. That is not the Finesse 2,000-user cluster line in the design guide. [cite:1] [cite:3]

13 · Interview

Oral answers

30 seconds. Webex CCE names two hybrid models in March 2022: SBC Peering and Localized RTP. I do not invent their call flows. The what’s-new page sends that detail to the partner technical guide.

2 minutes. Hybrid IVR, July 2023, is a different idea: some IVR nodes stay on-premises, and the example is OTP, while other tasks use Dialogflow CX. A static CX message can cover up to 30 seconds. Regional media, July 2024, keeps audio in the caller’s region. In the U.S. tenant example, control signals are what travel home. VPN-less Finesse does not include FIPPA or several desktops behind one NAT. Custom SIP headers in the August 2023 standalone note ride the initial INVITE only.

30 seconds, scale. Forty-eight thousand concurrent agents and 300 calls per second is a January 2025 single-tenant sentence. Two thousand is the Finesse cluster line in another guide.

2 minutes, peering. The January 2025 early-access paragraph says Equinix Cloud Exchange or Megaport, and then says a private circuit through Virtual Connect via VPN. I leave that sentence intact and I design from the September 17, 2026 Edge Connect article, which calls it private peering to Meetings, Calling, and Webex Contact Center Enterprise data centers. Digital channels also need a date: email, chat, and SMS in July 2023; WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger in the January 2025 block. Every nhy3hvdb section I used is older than twelve months, so those rows stay re-check.

What you will not say. You will not draw Localized RTP as Virtual Connect, and you will not treat the partner-guide pointer as a configuration you have already read.
