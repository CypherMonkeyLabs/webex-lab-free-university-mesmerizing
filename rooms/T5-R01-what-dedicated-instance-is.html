T5 · Dedicated Instance

What Dedicated Instance Is

Dedicated Instance is a Webex Calling add-on: a dedicated cloud instance based on Unified CM. Calling features are not harmonized with multi-tenant Webex Calling. Peering design lives in the next rooms.

Room T5-R01 Version v0.1 last-verified: TODO Difficulty L2 Est. time 80–100 min Prereq T2-R02 Reading 20 min

Key takeaways

  • The add-on includes Unified CM, optional IM and Presence, Unity Connection, Expressway, multiple clusters, Emergency Responder in the Americas only, and optional Session Management Edition. [cite:1]
  • A full cluster is up to 8 call-processing servers or about 50,000 devices on version 14 and above. Do not activate call processing on the TFTP, MOH, or publisher nodes Cisco did not activate. [cite:1]
  • Partner or customer limits: 50,000 configured devices in a full-size cluster, and a maximum of 160,000 end users configured or synchronized over LDAP. [cite:1]
  • Webex Calling users cannot be part of a hunt group on Dedicated Instance. The features are not harmonized. [cite:1]

01 · Executive summary

Five lines, then why it matters

  • Knowledge Workers and Common Area devices captured in the First Time Setup Wizard determine how many call-processing nodes Cisco deploys. Sizing is a function of the Dedicated Instance team, not of the old on-premises cluster size. [cite:1]
  • The first cluster in a region is activated within 48 hours. Later clusters in that region are activated within 5 business days. More than three clusters in a region need customer-provided subnets. Cisco-provided subnets support only up to three. [cite:1]
  • SME is eligible when three clusters are deployed. One SME can span regions. Its node-to-node and database replication traffic uses the Cisco backbone. [cite:1]
  • Nightly backups keep the latest three in the data center. They are for disaster recovery. You do not get access, and they are not a change-backout strategy. [cite:1]
  • Base configuration creates interop objects whose names start with patterns such as WXC-DI. Cisco says not to modify or delete the default trunks. The base-configuration article was updated May 27, 2025. [cite:2] re-check
Why it matters. People hear “Webex Calling” and put Dedicated Instance users into a multi-tenant hunt group. The introduction article gives that exact example as something that does not work. Peering math belongs in T5-R02 and T5-R03.

03 · Core concepts

A private Unified CM, still inside Webex Calling

Add-on, not a second product family

Webex Calling includes a dedicated cloud instance based on the Cisco Unified Communications Manager architecture. It is integrated with Webex Calling and is an add-on. It supports the same features as on-premises Unified CM, including Jabber and Webex App, and it can trunk to third-party PBXs. It is orderable as an add-on for Webex Calling Flex Plan 3.0 through partners in specific countries. See the Global Availability Guide for countries. [cite:1]

Calling features are not harmonized

Customers can place users on both platforms. The introduction says the calling features are not harmonized. The example is that Webex Calling users cannot be part of a hunt group on Dedicated Instance. [cite:1]

Call processing stays on the nodes Cisco activated

A cluster is a publisher plus subscribers. The publisher role named in the sizing section is provisioning. Other subscriber roles include TFTP, MOH, and call processing. A fully deployed cluster supports up to 8 call-processing servers or approximately 50,000 devices on version 14 and above. Do not activate call processing on TFTP, MOH, or publisher nodes other than the nodes Cisco activated. [cite:1]

Backups are not yours

Cisco takes a backup of all Dedicated Instance UC applications every night and keeps the latest three in the data center. They are password protected and used only to restore after disaster recovery. On a major hardware failure the cluster can be reinstalled and restored from the daily DRS backup. Restore time depends on database size and which components you restore. This is not a change-backout strategy, and you will not have access to these backups. [cite:1]

13 · Interview

Oral answers

30 seconds. Dedicated Instance is a Webex Calling add-on built on Unified CM. It includes Unity Connection, Expressway, optional IM and Presence, optional SME, and Emergency Responder only in the Americas. Calling features are not the same as multi-tenant Webex Calling. A Webex Calling user cannot sit in a Dedicated Instance hunt group.

2 minutes. Cisco sizes the cluster from Knowledge Workers and Common Area devices, not from the old on-premises bill of materials. A full cluster is up to eight call-processing servers or about 50,000 devices on version 14 and above. I do not turn on call processing on the publisher or on TFTP and MOH nodes Cisco left inactive. I can configure 50,000 devices and up to 160,000 end users, including LDAP users who are only there for directory lookup. The first cluster in a region comes up within 48 hours. Later ones within five business days. Past three clusters I bring subnets. SME waits until I have three clusters, and its replication stays on the Cisco backbone.

30 seconds, operations. Nightly backups keep the latest three. I cannot download them to undo a mistake. MRA starts at 20 percent of Knowledge Workers even though the entitlement is 100 percent. B2B entitlement is 5 percent. Expressway-E blocks inbound B2B until I change the call policy.

2 minutes, the boundary. Default interop trunks use the WXC-DI name pattern, one toward each data center, and I do not edit them. The location needs a PSTN connection and a main number or the route group is unusable. RedSky objects are x-prefixed and the partner fills in the account ID. Peering, ASN 13445, and the choice among Partner Connect, Virtual Connect, and Edge Connect are the next two rooms. The introduction I am quoting was updated September 23, 2026. The base-configuration names come from May 27, 2025, so those rows stay re-check.

What you will not say. You will not say the platforms share hunt groups, and you will not treat a DRS backup as your rollback plan.
