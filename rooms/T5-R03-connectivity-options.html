00 · Header

T5 · Dedicated Instance

Connectivity Options

Three official articles describe how a Dedicated Instance region is reached: Partner Connect, Virtual Connect, and Edge Connect. Each one assumes the region is already activated. T5-R02 keeps the peering deep dive.

Room T5-R03 Version v0.1 last-verified: TODO Difficulty L3 Est. time 90–110 min Prereq T5-R01 Reading 22 min

Key takeaways

  • Partner Connect lets a partner aggregate customer networks. Direct cross-connect is 1 G or 10 G. Equinix Fabric is 200 MB to 10 GB. Activate Dedicated Instance in the region first. [cite:1] re-check
  • Virtual Connect is two GRE-over-IPsec tunnels per region, 250 Mbps each. Failover puts all traffic on one tunnel, so combined traffic must stay at or under 250 Mbps. [cite:2] re-check
  • Edge Connect is private peering through Equinix or Megaport. Webex public BGP ASN is 13445. A Dedicated Instance link requires the redundancy checkbox and two connections to different data centers. [cite:3]
  • Edge Connect 3.0 can carry multi-tenant, Dedicated Instance, and Meetings on one peering capability. Cisco still requires peering at two sites in a region. [cite:4] re-check

01 · Executive summary

Five lines, then why it matters

  • Partner Connect addressing is a public /31 or /30 per physical link, four subnets per geo for Direct and four for Equinix Fabric, a public or private ASN, and a /16 per geo for partner management of the UC applications. Link subnets are not routed outside the service. [cite:1]
  • Partner Connect cannot carry Webex Calling and Meetings unless the customer also has the Dedicated Instance add-on. To carry those as well, the partner hosts a NAT server and an extra Dot1Q connection. The datacenter timeline is 10 business days. [cite:1]
  • Virtual Connect private ASNs come from 64512 through 65534. Cisco assigns a public but not Internet-routable /24, split into two /25s, one per data center. The CPE advertises a default route on each tunnel. [cite:2]
  • Only a Customer Full admin activates Virtual Connect. A Customer read-only admin can view status. One CPE with two tunnels is supported. Two CPEs is the recommended design. [cite:2]
  • Link-priority communities on the current Edge Connect article run from 13445:200 through 13445:900, and 900 is the most desirable path. T5-R02 is the room that already walks the two-link rule in more depth. [cite:3]
Why it matters. The three options share words like peering, Equinix, and BGP, and they do not share a bandwidth ceiling or a failure model. A 250 Mbps Virtual Connect tunnel is not a 10 G Partner Connect cross-connect, and neither sentence deletes the two-site Edge Connect rule.

03 · Core concepts

Same region, three contracts

Partner Connect

The partner aggregates customer networks and extends them to Dedicated Instance. Two options: a direct physical cross-connect at an Equinix facility, with VRF and BGP, or Equinix Cloud Exchange Fabric, a virtual connection from any Equinix Fabric location. Direct facilities are listed for North America, Europe, Australia, and APJC. Activate Dedicated Instance in the region before the peering request. [cite:1] re-check

Virtual Connect

An add-on. Point-to-point IP VPN over the internet using existing CPE. Each order in a region is two GRE-over-IPsec tunnels, one to each redundant data center. Bandwidth is 250 Mbps per tunnel and the article recommends it for smaller deployments. All cloud traffic passes the customer headend, so it may not suit many remote sites. Cisco hosts the redundant tunnels and internet access in the Dedicated Instance data center. The customer provides internet bandwidth, public IPs for two IPsec tunnels, and GRE transport addresses. Devices must support BGP and GRE over IPsec. [cite:2] re-check

Edge Connect still wants two Dedicated Instance connections

The current article says Edge Connect bypasses the public Internet and connects to Webex Meetings, Webex Calling, and Webex Contact Center Enterprise data centers, through Equinix or Megaport. For an Edge Connect for Dedicated Instance link, the redundancy checkbox is mandatory, and the second connection goes to a different Webex data center in the region. A customer-story article says Edge Connect 3.0 can consolidate multi-tenant, Dedicated Instance, and Meetings over a single peering capability, and the same article says Cisco requires peering at two sites in a region. Keep both sentences. [cite:3] [cite:4]

APJC “Q1CY2020” is stale wording

The Partner Connect table says Fabric has 30 or more points in the Americas and EMEAR, and that APJC is targeted for Q1CY2020. That date is in the article retrieved on 2026-10-14. Treat the APJC timing as re-check, not as a current forecast. KSA partner connectivity options are stated as currently unavailable. [cite:1]

04 · Ecosystem

What rides these links

Glossary

Partner Connect
Partner-aggregated private connection. Direct cross-connect or Equinix Fabric. [cite:1]
Direct cross-connect
Physical connection at Equinix. Charge is the physical connection. Two points per listed geo. 1 G or 10 G. [cite:1]
Equinix Fabric
Virtual connection. Charge for the physical connection and each virtual connection, one virtual connection per customer. 200 MB to 10 GB. [cite:1]
Dot1Q
Partner gear must support Layer 3 subinterfaces with dot1q. The partner provides a tag when activating a customer. [cite:1]
Virtual Connect
Two GRE-over-IPsec tunnels per region over the customer’s internet. Hub design. [cite:2]
Dual-tier headend
Virtual Connect routing and GRE on one device, IPsec on another. [cite:2]
/25 per data center
Cisco splits the assigned /24 into two /25s and advertises each over its tunnel. [cite:2]
Edge Connect
Private peering that bypasses the public Internet. Equinix or Megaport. ASN 13445. [cite:3]
Link-priority community
13445:200 through 13445:900. 900 is the most desirable path. AS-path prepending is not supported with private ASNs. [cite:3]
Edge Connect 3.0
The customer-story article says one peering capability can carry multi-tenant, Dedicated Instance, and Meetings. Two sites in the region are still required. [cite:4]
BFD
Default 300 ms times 3 on Edge Connect for Dedicated Instance links, and the same default on Meetings and Calling links. [cite:3]

13 · Interview

Oral answers

30 seconds. Dedicated Instance has three connectivity articles. Partner Connect is the partner’s aggregated private connection. Virtual Connect is two GRE-over-IPsec tunnels over the internet. Edge Connect is private peering through Equinix or Megaport. I activate the region before I request the first two.

2 minutes. Partner Connect Direct is a 1 or 10 gigabit cross-connect. Fabric is 200 megabits to 10 gigabits, and each customer needs a virtual connection. Addressing is a public /30 or /31, four subnets per geo, and a /16 for managing the UC apps. I cannot use Partner Connect for Calling and Meetings unless Dedicated Instance is on the order, and then I still add NAT and another Dot1Q tag. The data-center work is quoted as 10 business days. KSA is called out as unavailable.

30 seconds, Virtual Connect. Two tunnels, 250 Mbps each, and the sum also stays at 250 Mbps because a failure lands everything on one tunnel. Cisco gives me a private ASN from 64512 to 65534 and a /24 cut into two /25s. My CPE sends a default route.

2 minutes, Edge Connect. Webex ASN 13445. My peering subnet is a public /30 or /31. I do not advertise RFC 1918. If I use a private ASN, Webex strips it, so I steer with communities from 13445:200 up to 13445:900. A Dedicated Instance link must check redundancy and land on two data centers. Edge Connect 3.0 can put multi-tenant, Dedicated Instance, and Meetings on one peering capability, and the article that says so still requires two sites. Partner Connect and Virtual Connect were updated in 2025, so those rows stay re-check. The Edge Connect header I used is September 17, 2026.

What you will not say. You will not put the 250 Mbps ceiling on an Edge Connect order, and you will not drop the second site because a sentence said “single peering.”
