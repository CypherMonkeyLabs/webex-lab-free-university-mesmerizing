00 · Header

T5 · Dedicated Instance

DI Admin & Operations

Dedicated Instance operations is a RACI, not a shared login. Cisco builds and restores the cloud UC applications. The customer or partner owns the dial plan, the users, premise DNS, and the service restart — after they tell Cisco.

Room T5-R04 Version v0.1 last-verified: TODO Difficulty L3 Est. time 80–100 min Prereq T5-R02 Reading 16 min

Key takeaways

  • If there is no active partner, the customer takes the partner column. [cite:1]
  • Advertise 0.0.0.0/0 toward the Dedicated Instance data center. More specific routes are not accepted. [cite:1]
  • Do not edit the base UC configuration Cisco deployed. Dial plan and integrations are the customer or partner column. [cite:1]
  • TAC cannot restart a UC service. Notify Cisco first, then restart. [cite:1]
  • Planned maintenance that disrupts calling gets at least 10 calendar days of written notice. The regional window itself cannot be moved. [cite:2]

01 · Executive summary

Five lines, then why it matters

  • R means the person doing the work. A signs off. C is two-way. I is one-way. [cite:1]
  • Cisco is accountable and responsible for the core application build, core monitoring, and operational readiness testing before handover. [cite:1]
  • A widespread issue is 100 percent of a UC cluster, or more than one cluster. Cisco resolves non-configuration issues there and restores clusters. A smaller issue is the customer or partner’s configuration to fix. [cite:1]
  • Service requests live at Calling → Dedicated Instance → Service Request. Day-0 and Day-1 types include DNS, firewall, firmware upload, cloud connectivity, audit log, UC application management, DI–MT inter-op trunks, UC application lifecycle, and IaaS. [cite:3]
  • Open a TAC case on Collaboration and Conferencing, sub-technology UCM Cloud / Dedicated Instance. Do not choose the Webex option. Port 21 FTP is not allowed. [cite:4]
Why it matters. A design review fails when someone treats DRS as a customer rollback, adds a custom SAN, or restarts CallManager without the service request. Those three are written down.

03 · Core concepts

The split the matrix actually prints

RACI

Responsible does the task. Accountable signs off. Consulted is two-way. Informed is one-way. The matrix covers partner onboarding, customer onboarding, and operations. [cite:1]

No partner

In the absence of an active partner, the customer assumes the responsibilities designated for the partner. [cite:1]

Two version sentences

The RACI article, header October 09, 2026, says customers may stay on n-1 of the current UC applications deployed in Dedicated Instance, and it names v15SU4a, unless a security issue forces a COP or an upgrade. The partner operations guide prints a table with v14.0 as n-1 and v15.0-SU4a as n, then says that n-1 has entered end of support and those customers must upgrade. Quote both. Do not collapse them. The partner-operations markdown retrieved 2026-10-14 did not print a header date. re-check that table before you teach which release is n. [cite:1] [cite:2]

Base config is Cisco’s

Default base configurations in the UC applications are Cisco A+R. Customer or partner should not modify them. Non-base configuration, for example dial plan, integrations, and PSTN, is customer or partner A+R. [cite:1]

09 · Self-question ladder

Twelve rungs

L0What does Dedicated Instance operations decide first?

Which RACI column owns the activity: customer, partner, or Cisco. [cite:1]

L1What breaks if the partner column is empty?

Nothing in the matrix moves to Cisco. The customer assumes the partner responsibilities. [cite:1]

L1Why advertise a default route?

The Dedicated Instance side does not accept more specific routes than 0.0.0.0/0 from the customer. [cite:1]

L2Where is a Day-1 request opened?

Calling → Dedicated Instance → Service Request. [cite:3]

L2Who may restart a service?

The customer or partner, after they inform Cisco. TAC cannot. [cite:1]

L3How do you prove a fault is Cisco’s?

Show it is a non-configuration issue on 100 percent of a UC cluster, or across clusters. Smaller configuration faults stay with the customer or partner, while cluster restore stays with Cisco in both rows. [cite:1]

L3What evidence shows a maintenance window was announced?

Written notice at least 10 calendar days ahead for work that disrupts calling, sent to partners enrolled for Maintenance and outages. [cite:2]

L4How is certificate ownership different from multi-tenant Calling?

On Dedicated Instance, Cisco manages UC application certificates and rejects custom SANs. A UCM Cloud conversion keeps customer-managed certificates until the domain and CA transfer. This room does not restate multi-tenant trunk certificates. [cite:1]

L4Who owns CCP numbers versus the CCP link?

Customer or partner owns procurement and configuration. Cisco owns connectivity to the Cloud Connected PSTN provider. [cite:1]

L5Why will you not use DRS as the rollback for a dial-plan change?

The RACI says Cisco does not do on-demand restores and does not allow a restore as a change backout. Partners also have no DRS access. [cite:1] [cite:2]

L5Which n-1 sentence do you teach?

Both, until you re-read them. October 09, 2026 RACI allows n-1 of the deployed applications and names v15SU4a, with a security exception. The partner operations table prints v14.0 as n-1 and v15.0-SU4a as n, then says that n-1 is end of support. [cite:1] [cite:2]

L3What is the TAC technology pair?

Collaboration and Conferencing, then UCM Cloud / Dedicated Instance (DI). High severity is a 60-minute response target. Call frontline for P1 and P2. [cite:4]

13 · Interview

Oral answers

30 seconds. Dedicated Instance splits work with a RACI. Cisco builds and monitors the core applications and restores clusters. The customer or partner owns dial plan, users, premise DNS, and acceptance testing. No partner means the customer takes that column.

2 minutes. Connectivity advertises 0.0.0.0/0. More specific routes are refused. Base configuration stays as Cisco deployed it. Widespread means the whole cluster or several clusters, and Cisco takes non-configuration faults there. A single mis-set route pattern is still yours. You can restart a service only after the Control Hub request notifies Cisco. TAC cannot restart it. DRS is Cisco’s backup. It is not your backout, and you do not get the backups.

30 seconds, certificates. Cisco manages Dedicated Instance UC certificates and will not add a custom SAN. After renewal, SSO has to be disabled, the IdP metadata reimported, and SSO turned back on. Converted UCM Cloud customers keep their own certificates until the domain and CA move to Cisco.

2 minutes, the window. Disruptive planned maintenance gets at least 10 calendar days of written notice if the partner is enrolled for Maintenance and outages. The AMER weekday window is 9 p.m. to 6 a.m. Eastern and cannot be slid. You can ask for another Cisco date with a lifecycle service request, and you should answer in two or three days. A severe vulnerability or a certificate about to expire does not move. Do not vulnerability-scan the cloud yourself.

What you will not say. You will not merge the October 09, 2026 n-1 sentence with the partner-operations v14.0 table. You will not open the TAC case under the Webex product option. You will not claim port 21 FTP is allowed.
