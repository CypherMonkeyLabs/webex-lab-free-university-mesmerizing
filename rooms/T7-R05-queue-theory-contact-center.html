T7 · SIP, routing, and queues

Queue theory for contact center

Webex Contact Center routing patterns and Webex Calling hunt-group patterns. The Labs page Erlang model is a Canon study model. It is not estimated wait. Retrieved 2026-10-14.

Room T7-R05 Version v0.1 last-verified: TODO Difficulty L3 Est. time 100–130 min Prereq T3-R03 Reading 16 min

Key takeaways

  • Skill-based selection filters agents who entirely satisfy the contact or the queue, then applies the pattern. Weights change Best Available scoring. They do not change Longest Available. [cite:1]
  • Non-skill Longest Available picks the agent available longest since their last contact. An offer on any media drops that position for every channel. A small race can offer two contacts at once because the architecture is distributed. [cite:1]
  • Circular is round-robin with a pointer, and np2fdx lists it for agent-assignment queues. Top-down always starts at the first configured agent. There is no pointer. [cite:1]
  • Best Available sums proficiency of the skills in the requirement. A less-than-or-equal skill is scored as 10 minus the value. Boolean, text, and enum skills are not scored. [cite:1]
  • A Calling hunt group is a different object. Caps in o6rfjeb: Circular, Top Down, and Longest Idle 1,000 agents. Weighted 100. Simultaneous 50. [cite:2]

01 · Executive summary

Four patterns, and no Erlang formula

  • Skill-based selection filters agents who entirely satisfy the contact or the queue, then applies the pattern. Weights change Best Available scoring. They do not change Longest Available. [cite:1]
  • Non-skill Longest Available picks the agent available longest since their last contact. An offer on any media drops that position for every channel. A small race can offer two contacts at once because the architecture is distributed. [cite:1]
  • Circular is round-robin with a pointer, and np2fdx lists it for agent-assignment queues. Top-down always starts at the first configured agent. There is no pointer. [cite:1]
  • Best Available sums proficiency of the skills in the requirement. A less-than-or-equal skill is scored as 10 minus the value. Boolean, text, and enum skills are not scored. [cite:1]
  • A Calling hunt group is a different object. Caps in o6rfjeb: Circular, Top Down, and Longest Idle 1,000 agents. Weighted 100. Simultaneous 50. [cite:2]
Why it matters. Longest Idle on a hunt group is not Longest Available on a contact-center queue. The caps and the pointer are the difference you can cite.

03 · Core concepts

What the articles actually select

Eligibility, then pattern

np2fdx: skills and dynamic skills decide who is eligible. The routing pattern picks one agent from that set. [cite:1]

Longest available is cross-media

Agents lose the longest-available position across all channels when they are offered a contact of any media type. [cite:1]

Do not teach the Labs Erlang line as Webex

The Canon labs page labels A=6, N=8, probability of waiting about 0.357, and wait about 32.1 seconds as a study model. np2fdx does not print that formula. 04 · Ecosystem & sister technologies

Glossary, map, sisters, edges

Glossary

Longest Available
Eligible agent who has been available the longest since the last contact. [cite:1]
Cross-media position
Any offered media type clears the longest-available position on every channel. [cite:1]
Simultaneous offer
np2fdx: a small possibility that one longest-available agent is routed multiple contacts queued to the same queue at the same time, because the architecture is distributed. [cite:1]
Circular
Round-robin. The pointer moves to the next configured agent after a successful contact-surplus match. Parked contacts do not move that pointer. [cite:1]
Top-down
Every contact starts at the first configured agent and walks down. No pointer. [cite:1]
Best Available
Score from proficiency skills named in the requirement. [cite:1]
Inverted skill
A <= condition uses 10 minus the agent’s value so a lower skill scores as a stronger match. [cite:1]
Weights
Used in Best Available. Not used by Longest Available. [cite:1]
Unscored skills
Boolean, text, and enum skills filter eligibility. They are not in the score. [cite:1]
Skill relaxation
A later requirement overwrites the contact’s skill requirement on a timer so more agents can match. [cite:1]
Call distribution group
Teams at a level. If nobody matches, the contact parks, then the next group’s teams are added. [cite:1]
Capacity-based team
Voice to a DN and a capacity. No Agent Desktop. np2fdx names voicemail, answering machines, or hunt groups as the people or devices that answer. [cite:1]
Default on a team queue
ubg5qd, page header October 08, 2026: for a non-skill queue with team assignment, Longest available is the default. For a non-skill queue with agent assignment, Circular is the default, and that queue can hold a maximum of 1,000 agents. [cite:3]
Hunt group path
Control Hub Services > Calling > Features. [cite:2]
Advance after rings
o6rfjeb: you can enable Advance after a set number of rings for the routing choice. [cite:2]
AutoCSAT skill
nv7abhz, page header October 08, 2026: AutoCSAT can be added as queue routing criteria, alone or with other skills. It is system-controlled. [cite:4]

06 · Config

Where the pattern is chosen

  1. In Contact Center queues, choose the routing pattern the queue type allows. ubg5qd says non-skill team assignment defaults to Longest available. [cite:3]
  2. For skills, set requirements the agent must meet entirely. Add weights only if the pattern is Best Available. [cite:1]
  3. For a Calling hunt group, sign in to Control Hub, go to Services > Calling > Features, and pick one of the five routing options. [cite:2]
  4. If you enable Advance after a set number of rings, that ring count applies to the routing choice. [cite:2]

13 · Interview

Oral answers

30 seconds. WxCC filters on skills, then runs Longest Available, Best Available, Circular, or Top-down as that queue allows. Weights belong to Best Available. [cite:1]

2 minutes. Longest Available is time since the last contact, and any media offer clears it. Circular keeps a pointer. Top-down does not. Best Available inverts a <= skill as 10 minus the value and ignores boolean, text, and enum in the score. [cite:1] A Calling hunt group has different caps, and agent call-forward does not apply to its calls. [cite:2]

30 seconds, what this is not. It is not the labs Erlang model, and it is not an ICM skill group. Those stay marked or labeled.

2 minutes, capacity and AI. A capacity-based team rings a DN. People do not use Agent Desktop. [cite:1] AutoCSAT can be a system-controlled skill on the queue criteria. The what’s-new page header is October 08, 2026. [cite:4]

What you will not say. You will not invent a fifth pattern name.
