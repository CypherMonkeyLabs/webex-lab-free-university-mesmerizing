00 · Header

Basic quality management and recording

T9-R03. Basic QM is recording plus evaluation. The existing QM column adds workflows, calibration, appeals, and live screen monitoring. [cite:3]

Reading 9 minWrittenStudy notes

Key takeaways

  • Basic QM includes call recording, evaluation across voice and digital, search and playback, text search, screen recording, and evaluation forms. [cite:3]
  • Existing QM adds insights reporting, workflows, custom metadata, live screen monitoring, calibration, appeals, and HR or training tagging. [cite:3]
  • py6u9h names A-FLEX-WFO-NQM as a full QM SKU used by Performance Management, Enterprise Analytics, and Insights. [cite:4]

02 · Map

Basic QM is not the AI add-on

nmzg380’s quality package is recording and forms. nvtdfxk’s AI Quality Management is a later, paid add-on with its own SKUs. Keep the names apart. [cite:3] [cite:6]

03 · Core

What the opened pages say

Basic QM on nmzg380: call recording, evaluate contacts across voice and digital, view search playback and delete, text search in the body of a text, email, chat, or SMS, interaction contact details export, screen recording, create and edit evaluation forms, standard evaluation viewing, and contact import. [cite:3]

Table 1 says existing quality management is all of that plus interaction export, insights reporting, QM workflows, custom metadata, QM views, contact queue, live screen monitoring, evaluation calibration, evaluation appeal, and HR/training contact tagging. [cite:3]

py6u9h’s Performance Management row accepts full WFM, full QM, or the WFO bundle. The full QM name printed there is A-FLEX-WFO-NQM. [cite:4]
